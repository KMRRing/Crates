      Rules

      Gold dice are your lives and your roles. Plain dice are your money and your weight in the bidding. Faces: 1 Wild, 2 Banker, 3 Trader, 4 Auditor, 5 Legal, 6 Regulator. Everyone's dice stay hidden until something shows them.

      Your turn: one move, then raise the bid or call it. Before you bid you also get two free moves, once each: ask a player a question about their hand (you each learn the answer about the other), and claim Legal to reroll one of your dice, secretly (say, one that landed face up).

      Moves. Take a die from the bank (it lands face up for all to see). Reroll any of your dice, secretly. Hit: pay 7 dice and they lose a gold die (with 8 plain dice you must). Or claim a role your gold dice show, true or not.

      Roles. Banker takes 3 dice from the bank. Trader steals 2 from a player. Auditor looks at all of a player's dice. Regulator either learns how many dice on the table show a face, or pays 4 dice to sanction a player, who loses a gold die. Legal also blocks steals and sanctions, and Auditor blocks steals (a sanction's 4 dice are spent either way).

      Challenges. The player a claim or block is aimed at (or the next player) may challenge. As in Coup, whoever's wrong loses a gold die: the bluffer if caught, the challenger if the claim was true (and the proof is shown).

      Bids. A bid says "at least 6 dice on the table show 4", counting every die, everyone's, with 1s wild. Every turn ends with you either raising the standing bid (more dice, or as many of a higher face) or calling it. Only the player whose turn it is can call, and only the latest bid. The referee counts and says only whether it held: if it did, you (the caller) pay the bidder 3 dice; if not, the bidder pays you. Earlier bids in the same run aren't judged. The next player then opens a fresh bid.

      Out. Can't pay in full, lose a gold die as well. No gold dice, you're out. Last one in wins.
