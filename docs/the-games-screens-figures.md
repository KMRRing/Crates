# The games screen's figures (October 2026)

Each tile shows one figure, the same way for you and your partner (suite.js: markOf, shared by shareBests): a win streak, the longest (Crates, Cartel: noteStreak); today's time (Slate, Delta: noteDayTime, each with a Today's puzzle, today's date its seed, a medium board, the first solve timed); today's count (Deck's cards revised, Parley's lessons: noteDayCount); stars collected (Calibre, Harbour, Blend, Refinery: noteStars, Blend and Refinery three a level by margin against par); or a best. A game with a daily (Punt, Quote, Origin, Order, Chart) shows today's when either of you has played it, and otherwise the bests: Punt's from today's run or a preset with every difficulty, Chart's from everything worldwide, Order's without its History runs (the stories, once Stories); Order has a daily for each of its modes, and its tile shows today's best of them. The chess puzzle rush's 3 min has five seconds' grace a puzzle (README, Puzzle rush): the clock waits, then runs, and never gains.

Today's comes first (October 2026). While a game's daily is still to play today (Slate, Delta, Punt, Quote, Origin, Order, Chart), its tile opens straight onto it (the tile's link carries `#today`, which the game takes once and clears with `arrivedForToday` from suite.js): a fresh day's puzzle or run, or today's where you left it; a board or run you'd started is left only after the game asks, as it does from its menu. Once today's is played the tile opens the game as it was. Inside a game, the menu's Random/Today's choice starts on Today's while it's still to play, and Origin and Order, after a finished run, deal today's next; a summary offers Today's when it's still to play. These came after a morning of dailies that didn't show: in Slate, Delta and Chart, today's was a choice in a list, and the board that opened was the last one or a random one, so it was easy to play a random board for the daily.

A daily counts the moment its result is final: Slate and Delta at the solve, Crates at the board's end, Order at its last card, Origin when its last country is found or given up, Chart at its last pin, Quote when its last quote settles, Punt when its last bet settles (an endless run when it ends). The summary that follows counts it again, which changes nothing; before, Origin, Chart, Quote and Punt counted only when that summary was opened, so leaving after the last answer lost the day.

Every daily goes by the same day: the UTC date, YYYYMMDD (days.js), the same set for everyone, rolling over at the same moment. Origin and Order (and Arb, since deleted) once counted days on the phone's own clock (days since 1970 by its calendar, 20734 for 8 October 2026), so the games screen never found their dailies, and they weren't the same set for everyone; what they kept moves to its date once, on the first page that opens (suite.js, with `movedToDates`), so a run played that day still shows. `tests/days.mjs` checks the day, the move and a day's best across Order's modes, and that no game counts days of its own again.

The figures sync like any game's save: today's times, streaks, counts and stars used to be written past the sync (they never left the device, and an older copy from elsewhere could win), and now go up with your solo code. The games screen redraws its tiles when a sync brings in something newer from your other devices, and passes it on to your partner's tiles.
