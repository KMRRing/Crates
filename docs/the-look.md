# The look

Brass instruments on a codex's page, with an Art Deco hand: the precision of a sextant or an astrolabe (brass on navy, engraved scales, enamelled detail) set the way the 1930s set gold on black, in wide geometric capitals and fine double rules. Light is the page: parchment (`--yard`), cream paper for cards and dialogs (`--sheet`), navy ink (`--ink`), brass for lines, frames and scales (`--brass`). Dark is the instrument itself: a night-navy dial, parchment lettering, bright brass. The four crate colours are enamels: ochre, verdigris (copper's green patina), cobalt and oxblood, and every game's own accent is retuned to an enamel of its colour, with its soft fill mixed from it and the paper (`color-mix`), so both schemes follow from one value.

All of it lives in `style.css`'s tokens (CLAUDE.md, "The look", lists them); a game names colours only for what is its own.
- Headings (a game's name in its header, dialog titles, menu headings, crate names, the tiles on the games screen) are Archivo at its widest (`--wide`, 125%), in spaced capitals: the font the suite already serves offline, so the look costs no download.
- Buttons are brass-framed with square corners; a primary action is a plate (`--plate`, `--plate-ink`: navy lettered in gilt on the page, brass lettered in navy on the dial), and every game's main action button is that plate. Tiles are paper with a brass edge.
- A game's stage (the card it plays on: Chart's map card, Quote's question, Manifest's floor) is a panel with square-ish corners framed in a double rule, drawn as an inset shadow so it sits under the content and never crosses a map or a board.
- Category labels are tags, not pills: small rectangles in the game's enamel. The only round things are dots, discs and round icon buttons.
- Every game's logo is its object drawn as the instruments are: navy, brass, parchment and its enamel. A logo's contour is drawn at its width times `--wire`: 1 in a game's header and on the games screen, 1.5 on the selected game, while the engraved detail keeps its fine weight.

The logo, the menu button and the loader are the Codex mark (README, "The mark"), and loading is drawn with it (`loading.js`). Whenever a page or a long task loads, the mark turns large in the middle of the screen, the task's words under it: a page's loader fades in only after a moment, so a page that's ready at once never shows it, and it lets taps through, so a page can never be locked behind it. A wait with stages says where it has got to: a bar fills under the words, a glint running along it so a slow stage never looks stuck, and `progress(words, at)` (`at` from 0 to 1 for the whole wait, the bar never going back) sets it, so every game shows stages without code of its own. A short wait inside a line or a box shows the tiny mark, turning (`loader-small.svg`; `waitMark()`); `busy(label)` returns the function that ends a large wait.

Two looks, chosen in Settings (More, Settings): Codex (the default) with a day and a night that follow the phone unless set, and Office, night only whatever the phone says (Modern and Kontor were removed on 4 November 2026, Korbi: "we only have one office theme (dark), and codex (light and dark)"). Office looks like Outlook at night: Microsoft's Fluent design, charcoal surfaces (`#1F1F1F` page, `#292929` cards), one blue (`#479EF5`, `#115EA3` for a plate) for what can be pressed and what's selected, Segoe UI in sentence case at 14 px, hairline borders, 4 px corners and dialogs with a soft shadow, no ornaments and no marks: the games screen is names with thin monochrome line icons (`OFFICE_ICONS` in `apps.js`, `class="k-ico"`), and each game's board (Pipes' tiles, Harbour's chart and 3D skin, Cartel's table, the chess board) has its Office night colours. Office lives in `themes.css`; `theme.js` sets the theme and mode on the page before it draws. The suite's dropdown (`dropdown.js`) reads at night in both: it takes a deep face of the logo's edge, lettered in the ink (letters in the logo's tint on a face of the same tint once made a choice like Refinery's crude unreadable at night).
