# The Appendix

The last section of the games screen, after Tools (2 November 2026, Korbi, after the library review: "arb, refinery, blend, timegrapher can be moved into a new section at the bottom of the game"). It holds what is set aside for now, so the main grid is the games that will stay in this app: **Arb** (flagged for removal: "weak"), **Refinery** and **Blend** (fun, but built on the biofuels trade, so not universal enough: each is to become an app of its own), **Timegrapher** (for the personal tier: tools for Korbi's own use, outside the public app) and **Brut** ("best retired"). Nothing in them changed: the tiles only moved, their ids, saves, bests and Random's lists are as they were, and they are still played from the games screen.

Brut is retired in two steps, so that nothing is lost on the way. Its tile is in the Appendix now. Its knowledge stays: the wine bank is Punt's Wine level too (162 cards), so anyone who wants to learn wine does it in Punt, as a topic rather than a course. What is left to do is to put the wine in `kb/` (the bank is edited in place today, not built from the graph, so a wine is not an entity that Chart, Quote and Punt's other levels can ask about) and trim it to what a general player would meet (the 64 wines added on 31 October lean towards Geneva, the Valais, Ticino, Franken and Württemberg, as the rest of the graph does, see the library overview), and only then remove the tile and the blind-tasting page. With it the Courses section is Calibre and Parley.

The Appendix is a section in `SECTIONS` in `apps.js` (a game is in it when its `sec` is `appendix`), so a game leaves it by changing one word.

Decided on the same day, not built: **Casino** and **Dandan** are each to be an app of their own (Casino because a simulated-gambling game carries its own age rating and the store's rules for it, whatever the chips are called; Dandan because it is the largest game here and is to have its engine perfected), so the main app's grid will lose their sections when they are split off.
