# The Appendix

The last section of the games screen (2 November 2026, Korbi, after the library review: "arb, refinery, blend, timegrapher can be moved into a new section at the bottom of the game"). It holds what is set aside for now, so the main grid is the games that will stay in this app: the six **Casino** games (Baccarat, Blackjack, Roulette, Sic Bo, Craps, Hold'em: "move all the casino games to the appendix"; each to become an app of its own, because a simulated-gambling game carries its own age rating and the store's rules for it, whatever the chips are called), **Refinery** and **Blend** (fun, but built on the biofuels trade, so not universal enough: each is to become an app of its own), **Guilloché** and **Timegrapher** (set aside too; Timegrapher is for the personal tier, tools for Korbi's own use outside the public app), **Banquet** (Chinese banquet hand games, played for cups; set aside on 4 November 2026 with the rest), **Pearl** (id `masyu`; 4 November 2026, Korbi: "pearls get annexed") and **Polder** and **Avalanche** (idle games; 4 November 2026, Korbi: "polder and avalanche annex", to be split off fully later), and **Fuel maths**, the fuels version of Speed maths (the generic one is a tile in Puzzles; its deck is in `speedfuels-data.js` and listed for review in `docs/speedfuels.md`). Nothing in the games changed when the tiles moved: their ids, saves and bests are as they were, and they are still played from the games screen.

The Appendix stays out of Random and out of Start a game over (Korbi, 3 November 2026, decided when the shell was reshaped): its games are in `LEFT_OUT` in `random-core.js`, with the reason, and `startOver` in `apps.js` skips the section. They keep their `roundOver` calls, which do nothing while Random leaves them out (`tests/random.mjs` lets the Appendix's games call it). A game is in the Appendix when its `sec` in `apps.js` is `appendix`; it leaves by changing that one word, and then belongs in `ROUNDS` or `LEFT_OUT` on its merits.

**Hidden** (4 November 2026, Korbi: "make it so appendix and all the apps in it don't appear unless the user is logged in as ?solo=KORBIRUN"): the games screen (tiles, search, stars) leaves the section out unless the device's solo code is `KORBIRUN` and Settings' "Content marked for removal" is Include (Korbi, same evening: with it on Exclude the Appendix is hidden even on that code; `appendixOpen()` in `apps.js`). The pages themselves still open by address.

**Music** (5 November 2026, Korbi: "Put music in appendix", with a course in political theory to come in instead): its tile and its 24 lessons are kept whole and out of sight; the live count is 107 until the new course's tile lands.

**Gone from it** (3 November 2026): Arb (flagged "weak"; what Nexus took from its engine is `kb-things.js`), Brut (retired: wine is learned in Punt's Wine level, 162 cards from `wine-bank.js`, and cards Brut had put on the pile are still asked in Deck as Wine; its blind-tasting flights went with the page and are in git's history; putting the wine in `kb/` is still to do) and Dandan (out of the suite, developed in its own repository, KMRRing/Dandan).
