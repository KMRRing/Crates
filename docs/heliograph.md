# Heliograph
A glass tube with a port in its frame and a chandelier of crystals hanging from a crown: you set up to six mirrors so white light, turned through a colour crystal or two, shatters the runs of one colour in the chandelier and brings what hangs under them down (a shot scores `10·shattered² + 10·fallen`). Two moves a shot (three on the first), turning is free. Classic is the endless game, the chandelier coming down a layer every six shots; Levels (3 November 2026) is six stations of four levels, each station bringing one new thing: a quota, lanterns, a prism, shutters, the sun, then those mixed, ending with a level that clears the chandelier. The pure engine is `heliograph-engine.js`, the levels `heliograph-levels.js`, the feature help `heliograph-help.js`.

## Rulings
- October 2026: Korbi asked for it in 3D, the grid gone, the light coming from above and the time spent setting mirrors to do something plain to see.
- October 2026: light through both colour crystals is two beams side by side, one in each colour; one beam in the last colour with a glow in the first didn't read as both to him.
- October 2026: aiming at the top layer from the side brought every string down at once and left no reason to aim anywhere else, hence the skirt: the top two layers are reached only from below.
- A drag that starts on another mirror turns the camera, as from empty space: it once moved whatever mirror it began on and spent a move (Korbi lost moves that way). A Skip button went the same month: turning a mirror off the colour crystals and firing does the same, free.

## Traps
- `heliograph:best` and `:run2` are Classic's score and game, and nothing else; the games screen reads the best. Levels keep to `heliograph:camp` (`at`, `stars`), `:lruns` (the game begun on each level), `:mode` and `:help` (features whose help was shown). A level's rules live in its saved game as `x`, and a game without `x` is Classic, which is how a game saved before Levels still opens: never give Classic an `x`.
- A level is dealt from eight seeds in `heliograph-seeds.js` that the engine's simple player (`plan(…, "best")`) wins. A change to the engine, to `plan` or to a level makes them stale; `node tests/heliograph-deals.mjs --find` writes them again, and that test fails until then. The simple player wins nearly every deal, so it proves a level possible and nothing about its difficulty: goals sit about where "keen" (the best of ten aims looked at) ends, and the stars are for people.
- The sun: a mirror high near the port keeps the light for about ±14° of slant; the sweeps of ±20° and more make a mirror left there lose it, which is the point of the level. Under a sun `plan` sets its first mirror high, on the light of the middle of the day.
- `themes.css` restyles any class containing `-go`, `-label`, `-tag`, `-cat`, `-chip` or `-note` (Kontor drew `.hg-goal` as a green primary button), so the goal row is `hg-aim` and the shot line `hg-state`.
- The help scenes are shots of the real engine and the numbers in their texts come from it; they show colours and never name one, since the enamels are the theme's. A feature's help opens once, when a level first has it, never in a watching frame.
- Dropped: one move a shot ("few hands": turning is free, so it changed little, and under the sun it crushed the level) and a lowered line ("tight": the engine still reads `x.line`, no level sets it).

## Verdict, 3 November 2026
Heliograph now has what an endless score chase lacked: goals, stars, a new way of aiming every four levels, a session that ends. Whether that earns the tile is not settled: only bots have played it (no person has played a level), touch was only tried as a mouse in Chromium, and setting a mirror in 3D by its handle is slow on a phone. Weak spots I can see: the prism and the sun change the planning more than the skill, a lantern hanging low is nearly free, and under every level the game is still Classic's one puzzle, rebuilding the light with two moves. Missing: people playing the first eight levels on phones, and goals tuned from what they do, not from the bots.
