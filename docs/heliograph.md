# Heliograph
A glass tube with a port in its frame and a chandelier of crystals hanging from a crown: you set mirrors so white light, turned through a colour crystal or two, shatters the runs of one colour in the chandelier and brings what hangs under them down (a shot scores `10·shattered² + 10·fallen`). Two moves a shot (three on the first), turning is free. Classic is the endless game, the chandelier coming down a layer every six shots. Levels (4 November 2026) is six stations of five hand-made puzzles, each station bringing one twist: where you cut (Heligoland), bolted crystals (Visby), the prism (Reval), the splitter (Bergen), lamps and signals (Danzig), cracked glass (Lübeck). The pure engine is `heliograph-engine.js`, the stations `heliograph-chapters.js` (rules in `heliograph-levels.js`), their designed answers `heliograph-answers.js`, the feature help `heliograph-help.js`, the sound `heliograph-sound.js`.

## Rulings
- October 2026: Korbi asked for it in 3D, the grid gone, the light coming from above and the time spent setting mirrors to do something plain to see.
- October 2026: light through both colour crystals is two beams side by side, one in each colour; one beam in the last colour with a glow in the first didn't read as both to him.
- October 2026: aiming at the top layer from the side brought every string down at once and left no reason to aim anywhere else, hence the skirt: the top two layers are reached only from below.
- A drag that starts on another mirror turns the camera, as from empty space: it once moved whatever mirror it began on and spent a move (Korbi lost moves that way). A Skip button went the same month: turning a mirror off the colour crystals and firing does the same, free.
- 4 November 2026, Korbi: "heliograph: the levels are a bit uninspired at the moment. take a step back, think about what twists this game needs to make it good, and then implement that." Hence Levels as authored puzzles (below), and these twists, each a thing you can see in the tube:
  - **Splitter**: half-silvered glass, fixed; light that meets it goes on and turns, so one shot makes two beams (`trace` follows a queue of beams; a leg's `t0` is how far along its branch it starts, so branches play side by side).
  - **Lamp**: lit by a beam carrying its colour (a clear lamp, any light); the beam stops there. Goals: every lamp lit (kept shot to shot), all in one shot (`together`), or a signal (`order`: lit in the order listed, a lamp lit out of turn waits).
  - **Bolted crystal**: never falls, and holds its string; only its own colour hitting it removes it.
  - **Cracked (frail) crystal**: the first hit of its colour cracks it and the beam ends there; the second shatters it. Two beams in one shot are two hits. A crystal that falls needs no second hit.
  - **Bolted mirror** (`pins`): turns, never moves, and is not one of the mirrors in hand.
- A level is not a deal: its chandelier, colour crystals (which are not spent) and tools are fixed, the answer is designed, and the stars are for shots (the answer's length is three stars, one more is two).

## Traps
- `heliograph:best` and `:run2` are Classic's score and game, and nothing else; the games screen reads the best. Levels keep to `heliograph:camp2` (`at`, `stars`), `:lruns2` (the game begun on each level), `:mode`, `:help` (features whose help was shown) and `:prefs` (Sound). The first Levels (24 dealt levels) kept `:camp` and `:lruns`; those are left untouched and nothing reads them, so stars won there do not count in the new stations. A level's rules live in its saved game as `x`, and a game without `x` is Classic, which is how a game saved before Levels still opens: never give Classic an `x`.
- Changing a level's chandelier, gems, fixtures or the engine can make its answer stale: `tests/heliograph-levels.mjs` plays every answer and fails until it is found again. The simple players (`plan`) do not use splitters, bolted mirrors or more than two mirrors, so they fail the levels that need them: that is the grade, and the test holds the best of them to three stars on only a few levels.
- `x.hang` writes a chandelier by place (`"q,r"`) and string, top first, a digit for each crystal, then `b` (bolted), `f` (frail) or `l` (lantern). A frail crystal that hangs below another that can be cut falls with it and is never hit twice: put frail crystals where nothing above them can go, at the top of a string.
- A level of lamps alone has `hang: {}`; `fire` does not call that "cleared".
- The sun is still in the engine (`x.sun`; a mirror high near the port keeps the light for about ±14° of slant) but no level has it: it changed the planning more than the skill. Quota goals (`kind: "score"`) are still in the engine and in `starsOf`; no level has one.
- `themes.css` restyles any class containing `-go`, `-label`, `-tag`, `-cat`, `-chip` or `-note` (Office draws `.hg-goal` as a primary button), so the goal row is `hg-aim` and the shot line `hg-state`.
- The help scenes are shots of the real engine and the numbers in their texts come from it; they show colours and never name one, since the enamels are the theme's. A feature's help opens once, when a level first has it, never in a watching frame.
- Dropped: one move a shot ("few hands": turning is free, so it changed little) and a lowered line ("tight": the engine still reads `x.line`, no level sets it).
