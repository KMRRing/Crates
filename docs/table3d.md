# Table 2.0 (the card games' shared table)

`table3d.js`, `table3d.css`, `table3d-view.js` and `cards.js`/`cards.css`. Cards stay DOM; the table gets the depth. Hearts, Big Two, Landlord, Gin rummy, Boast and Hold'em get it by being on `.tk-mat` (the tipped cloth); Contract is the first to use it flat (`.tk-surface`).

**What it is**
- *Surface* (CSS only, painted once): a wooden rail (grain image, sheen), a brass inlay line and groove, the cloth inside it (weave, fibres, vignette, lamp pool). Tokens `--tk-w1..3` (rail), `--tk-inl` (inlay), `--tk-felt` (the game sets it on its `#app`); Codex day and night, and Office (graphite rail, steel inlay). Add the class `tk-surface` to any box (it needs room: set `--tk-rail` and pad by it; its direct children are lifted above the cloth).
- *Cards* (`cards.css`, every game): thickness and edge (`--cd-thick`; a game that sets its own `box-shadow` on `.cd` should include it), paper grain, an engraved back (navy panel, gilt lattice and rosette), court faces with an emblem (`.court.rk-J/Q/K`; `cardHtml` adds them). A card lying on the cloth (`.tk-mat .tk-centre`, `.tk-pile`) gets a contact shadow by itself; elsewhere add `box-shadow: var(--cd-thick), 0 .16em .38em rgba(0,0,0,.42)`.
- *Motion* (`createMotion`): `fly` ends in `settle(el)` (a card comes down and gives a little); `sweep(items, to)` now gathers the cards into a stack, holds a beat, then carries them off. Both do nothing under reduced motion, in a hidden page, under a pause or in a watching frame.
- *Parallax* (`createParallax(el)`, started by `createCloth`): sets `--tk-px` and `--tk-py` (-1 to 1) on `el` from the phone's tilt or the mouse; the lamp, the sheen and the tipped plane read them. Off under reduced motion, Settings' 2D mode and a battery under 20% not charging. It never asks for iPhone tilt permission, so there the mouse is all it has. Tests of taps: the transforms are real, so hit-testing stays true (`tests/table3d.mjs`; check cards at centre and edge in a browser).
- *View choice*: `tableView(game, flat, label3d)`; `tableView("contract", ["flat", "Flat"], "Table")`.

**Adopting it**
1. Tipped cloth: nothing to do; `createCloth` is already Table 2.0. Remove any `box-shadow` your CSS puts on `.cd` in the lanes.
2. Flat table: link `table3d.css`, put `tk-surface` on the table's box, set `--tk-felt`, pad for `--tk-rail`, give light text on the cloth (`--tk-text`), and start `createParallax(box)` when the table view is on (dispose it on the way back to flat). Dress your own pieces in your own CSS under one class (Contract: `#app.bd3`, `contract-3d.css`) so Flat is untouched. Play moves: `createMotion().fly` with `land: el` for a card played, `sweep` for a trick taken; redraw only when the markup changed (`fill`), or a card in the air is reset.
3. Next: Tarot (its trumps are SVG: add `.cd`-like thickness to them and use `--cd-thick`; the dog and the taker's pile on the cloth), the Trumps games (one page, three cloths: `--tk-felt` per game; their tipped `trumps-3d.css` can go on `.tk-mat`), Solitaire's DOM view (cards sit on a `tk-surface`; its three.js board stays), Shoe (a `tk-surface` with a shoe and the dealing flights).
4. Watch for: text on baize needs light tokens (suit colours too: black suits cream, red `#FF9C8C`); anything `overflow: hidden` on the surface clips the rail; the surface's `::before/::after` are positioned, so a grid or flex container is unaffected but its children need `position: relative` (the kit does it for direct children); `.cd.court` hides its big pip.
