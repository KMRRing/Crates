## Facts stated in the words, to confirm

- **text** Indenture: "about 1.6 million Indians, 1834 to 1920; other totals of 1.3 to 2 million"; "some ships lost over 17 percent; mortality among new arrivals in Mauritius sometimes over 20 percent a year" (a Wikipedia table summarising secondary work; Tinker, Northrup).
- **text** Chinese coolie trade: about 125,000 to Cuba (1847 to 1874) and 90,000 to 100,000 to Peru; Macau's trade banned 1874.
- **text** Voyages database figures (12.5 million embarked, 10.7 million disembarked, 12.1 percent mortality, 9 to 19.5 percent range) were read on slavevoyages.org on 4 November 2026; Curtin 1969 at about 9.6 million disembarked.
- **text** Haiti: 150 million francs cut to 90 million, debt service to 1947; the 22 billion dollar modern value is a New York Times study and is described as disputed.
- **text** Kültepe "many thousands of tablets" (outline: about 23,000, count unchecked); Uluburun "roughly ten tonnes of copper, about a tonne of tin" (the outline's 348 and 120 ingot counts were left out).
- **text** Suez: 20,000 to over 100,000 deaths "in the older literature, none reliable"; Congo 1 to 15 million with about 10 million usual (Hochschild; Louis and Stengers, Mignon, Vansina, Ndaywel).
- **text** Cuba Commission 1874; Putumayo (numbers left out); Zanzibar clove plantations 1840s; Allen's 570,000 to 730,000 in the Indian Ocean 1500 to 1850; the Indian Ocean East Africa total of about 8 million is stated as an estimate.
- **text** India's share of world manufacturing (Bairoch 25 to 2 percent), Maddison 24 percent in 1700, Patnaik 45 trillion dollars: each stated as an argued figure with who disputes it.
- **text** Chapter 14: Hormuz about 20 million barrels a day in 2024, about a fifth of petroleum liquids (EIA); Suez transits 57 to 64 percent below 2023 in 2025 and container ships 86 percent below in Q4 2025 (BIMCO, 30 January 2026). The 2026 Hormuz disruption is stated with no figure and a pointer to EIA. Re-pull every figure the week it is shown.
- **text** The Malacca strait "2.8 km at its narrowest" and Bab-el-Mandeb "about 30 km" come from kb/ notes.
- **text** The compliance lesson (14c) states how a biofuel counts toward a rule with a certificate and mass-balance accounting. Korbi's desk should read it: it takes no view on any rule.
