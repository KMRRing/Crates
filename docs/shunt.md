# Shunt

Gears shoved onto a hex plate (radius 2, 19 cells, 30 lever tabs). Meshed gears form trains; an odd loop jams.

So the 3D board is a watch movement under a round case (a ruby is a domed bearing with a hole in a silver chaton, in both views), and the case may lean with the phone (the later rulings changed the spring, the gold and the perlage).
- 4 Nov 2026, Korbi: "the guilloche is noisy. the outer rim is nice, but replace the clous de paris on the ring with just a fine brushed sunburst. also the arrows are offputting. instead, have little flat bits if it's the flat end of a hexagon or little triangle bits if its a corner of a hexagon, no arrow, the user will know the direction because of the face of the hexagon the bit is next to. those bits should be a slightly different texture, that's what the user presses ultimately." So the plate's field is brushed, not clous de Paris, and a lever is a small steel tab against the face it feeds, no arrow, in both views. Every entry feeds an outer face of the board (a cell with nothing behind it), so all thirty are flat bars parallel to their face; none is at a corner, so there are no triangles.
- 4 Nov 2026, Korbi: "shunt: the user should be able to rearrange the camera without it snapping back automatically. the gears need more differentiated colours. when they start blinking red because they're jammed, it's sort of hard to distinguish what is what. to counteract this, the watch case has to be more neutral: remove the blue dots and make it all silver. those black dots that make up the background of the hexagon are a bit offputting as well." So the camera stays where it is put (drag turns and tilts, pinch zooms, a Level button or a double tap goes home), gears are gilt, blued steel, rose gold and black, a jam is an oxblood ring and a red seat edge round a gear that keeps its colour, and the case, tabs and screws are silver in every theme, the seat floors a plain matte silver.

Traps:
- A run is the level plus its integer moves (-1 swap, 0..29 shove), replayed by `play()`; never store a board. Levels carry a stored beam line `sol` that `tests/shunt.mjs` replays: change a rule and regenerate them.
- The view is behind `createView` (`draw`, `animate`, `hit`, `leverAt`, `setMotion`, `dispose`): `shunt-view-2d.js` or the 3D watch `shunt-view-3d.js` (its shapes `shunt-3d-geo.js`, its engraving `shunt-3d-tex.js` made with `guilloche-engine.js`, its pure parts `shunt-3d-model.js`, its sounds `shunt-sound.js`); state and input mapping stay in `shunt.js`. A lever tab stands where the rules put it (the entry's cell and the way it shoves: `LEVERS` in 2D, `LEVER_W` in 3D, both derived, never placed by hand); where a touch means it is another matter (`hx`, `hy`): the outline zigzags, so tabs crowd under half a cell apart where two faces meet, and touch points are spread an equal angle apart on a circle, with a touch on a tab's own bar always its own (`leverAtPoint`). `shunt:tilt` (the case leans with the phone, a separate opt-in) is asked for in the menu, never at start: iOS wants its permission asked from a tap. `shunt:cam` is the 3D view's turn, tilt and zoom, written only when the player moves it (the home view is never written, `null` removes it); taps are found through the camera's plane at any angle (`planeAt`). `shunt:view` is written only when the player picks a view (`mode3d.js`: the default is never written down); a watching frame stays 2D.
- `suite.js` MARKS has `shunt: "daily"`, so the tile counts today's plate once judged.
- Every `shunt:` key is read through `shunt-keep.js`, which drops a value that is not this build's: `suite.js` syncs keys from old builds and devices, and the old wagon game's `shunt:seen` was `true`, which blanked the page (`new Set(true)`). `shunt:ver` 3 drops that game's `run`, `best` (in thousands) and `scrapped`.
- Never put "-go", "-chip", "-tag" in a class name: Office's wildcard rules in `themes.css` restyle them (`sh-goal` became a green bar).
- Tooth counts are multiples of 6 so neighbours interleave; lever pitch is about 0.47 D, so a lever is previewed first and confirmed by a second tap.
- Heavy search checks live in `tests/shunt-search.mjs` (about 20 s; nightly only).
- `vendor/three.js` is trimmed (no textures, environment maps, perspective camera, point or spot lights, PCF-soft shadows): the 3D view's grain and shading are vertex colours, the metals' reflection is added in the material's shader (`envify`), the camera is orthographic. Gear types are told apart by finish and by their window count (`WINDOWS`, both views), not by colour alone; a jam never recolours a gear. Check a new three.js class against the bundle's export list before using it.
- Each pitch has its own layer height (`LAYER_Y`) so unmeshed neighbours of different pitches pass over one another; the idler is as tall as all three layers. A bridge's two parts turn at their own pitch's speed, not as one body. Gears are placed in the 2D view's angles (tooth 0 on the x axis, the half-tooth phase for the other turning direction): `tests/shunt-3d.mjs` checks the teeth interleave.
