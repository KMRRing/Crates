# Head to head
A head-to-head on one deal (October 2026), for the games otherwise played alone: everyone at the table gets the same match (the game's seed and options), plays it through on their own phone, and sees the others' scores as they go. It's how Chess's puzzle rush (a puzzle battle), Blend, Survey, Pipes and Refinery play together (Origin, Order and Manifest did for a day in October 2026, until Korbi asked for Origin and Order as one team and Manifest either way: see their sections). `duel.js` is the table, on `together.js` like every duo match: asked for from the partner sheet like any other (a new table for each yes), or started from a Party, where it seats up to six (the request's `people`); Blend and Refinery are for a pair only, kept off Party's list as too long and quiet for a table of friends. A lobby lists who's at the table, each saying Ready; once everyone at the table is, a three-second countdown to a start they all share (the database's time, through `serverOffset`), and the game deals the match on each phone at that moment, so nobody sees it early. Anyone ready can start without waiting for those who aren't; a player who comes to a match already under way plays it on a countdown of their own, and the match is over when every run that began is done and nobody at the table is still to play it. Each phone reports its run as it changes (`runs/s<slot>`, not by number, which the database would read back as a list with holes: the score, a few words for the others, done, the time), and a finished run stands, so a reloaded phone can't change it; a report that changes nothing isn't sent (Blend's every step; Survey's chips are sent at most every second and a half). The others' scores show in a line the game places (under the header, or in its own score line), which opens the sheet. The result ranks everyone by the game's order (the score, then the time, unless the game says otherwise; equal runs share a rank, and a draw is a win for nobody in the pair's record), with the game's own lines under it (the chess rush's missed puzzles), Rematch (a new deal, the one who asked already ready) and Back to solo. A game keeps its solo run out of the match: its run, bests and today's stay as they were, and the match's own run is kept apart (`<game>:duel`), so a reload picks it up where it was. `duel-core.js` is every change to the table, pure, tested in `tests/duel.mjs` through the database's own round trip; on the stand-in database two phones went through the lobby, the countdown, play, a reload mid-run, the result on both, the pair's record, Rematch and Back to solo, and a party of three through a draw.
