# Topple
The fourth idle game (October 2026; README, Idle): `topple.html`, `topple.js`, `topple.css`; the rules are `topple-engine.js` (pure, read its header first), the levels `topple-levels.js`, the 3D table `topple-3d.js` (flat fallback `topple-flat.js`), the sound `topple-sound.js`, feature help `topple-help.js`. Three pieces of standing dominoes, each one enamel, are laid on a hexagonal table of sixty-one; when a knock falls due you press a domino and slide it along its axis and what it knocks falls, scoring ten times the dominoes felled times the steps they take. A bell on the table adds a flat 500 to a fall that passes it. **Classic** is the plain game; **Levels** is fourteen tables, each beaten at a goal score.

## Traps
- **The 3D table silently fell back to flat** once: `topple.js` takes any throw in `createTable` for "no WebGL", and the vendored `vendor/three.js` holds only the classes the pages use (`TorusGeometry` was not one). `tests/topple.mjs` reads `topple-3d.js` for every `new THREE.X` and fails on a class the bundle lacks.
- **The flat view starts as Classic's rules (`FREE`)**, so its first `terrain(FREE)` must still build the floors: it once took that for "no change" and drew nothing at all in Classic. `tests/topple-flat.mjs`.
- **Saved games**: every random thing comes from the game's seed and the number of the draw, so a game opened again deals on as it would have. The draw that once dealt each knock's strength is still made and passed over (`force` is gone) for that reason; `migrate` carries version 4 and 5 games over, and `VERSION` 6 drops level saves of earlier ones. A piece is named by its hexagons from its first, not by the order the pieces are made in. The turns are kept with the game, the chosen domino as `topple:sel` (a partner watching sees the handle).
- **A watching frame reads the partner's copy and writes nothing**, so everything the screen shows is in `topple:` keys: `run`, `best`, `seen`, `knocked`, `sound`, `sel`, `mode`, `camp2`, `lruns2`, `floor`, `help`. Classic's best is Classic's alone; a level's score never enters `topple:best`.
- **`topple:knocked`** is set by the first knock ever made. Until then the knock is offered plain (no Skip, which would end a first game for nothing) and what turning is comes after the fall; anyone with a best or a star counts as having knocked. The first-game hint is on the empty table only: once a piece is down the plaque would cover it.
- **The strip's score stays at the figure before a knock until the dominoes have fallen**, then counts up (`drawStrip(shown)`, `landScore`). `epoch` voids what a fall had left to do when a new game or another level is dealt before it lands; the page's timers for a fall (`later`) and its animations stop with the menu and the help dialog, as the table does.
- **An iPhone opens an audio context at the end of a touch**, not the start: `sound.unlock()` is asked again at each touch until the context runs, and a context the phone suspended or interrupted is asked before a sound. `tests/topple-sound.mjs` counts the calls on a fake context; how it sounds has not been heard.
- **Every piece a level can deal must fit its empty table**: a piece that fits nowhere stays in the hand for ever, and the next hand is dealt only when the last is down, so it ends the game on the first hand (Two courts and Fenland did). `tests/topple-levels.mjs` checks every table.
- **Scores**: bell +500 and cups +1,500 are flat and come after the multipliers; a skip (+100%), a gilded hexagon and a crossing (a fall over a spring's throw, a portal, a stair or down a floor) multiply the fall's points, and a gilding and a crossing together double once, not twice. Balls are `BALL` (-2) in `cells`, so every test for a domino (a colour of 0 or more) is false for one.
- **Goals** were set at 1.35 times the mean median of the careful and bell players in `tests/topple-players.js`, and set again when crossings and cups changed the scores. `REACHED` in `tests/topple-reached.js` names a game of the careful player that reaches each goal; if the deal changes, find them again. Balance is Korbi's to call, not the harness's: it is a bot that searches every fall and plays better than most people.
- **A scene is only how a table looks** (`scene` in a level; the rules never read it). Birch is Classic's and the first two levels', and must stay pixel for pixel as it was.
- **A table of two floors**: a cell is `floor * 61 + hexagon`, a fall goes down a drop and never up, and what is drawn over the table asks the view where a hexagon's floor is (`baseOf`).
- **Feature help is derived from the engine** (`topple-help.js`: its scenes run on the game's own rules, its figures are read from the engine and the levels), so a picture cannot disagree with the game. Classic and a watching frame show none.
- **Thresholds nobody has tried on a phone**: how far a slide is a push (0.55 of a hexagon and 18 px), how far out the handle stands (0.74), a double tap (420 ms, 40 px), a long press (half a second, 8 px).
