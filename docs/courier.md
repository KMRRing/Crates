# Courier
A three-lane swipe runner on a Hanseatic quay (2 November 2026, in Blades of Brim's way), in `reckoning.html`, `reckoning.js`, `reckoning.css`; rules in `reckoning-engine.js` (pure, tested by `tests/reckoning.mjs`), drawing in `reckoning-3d.js`, every model in `reckoning-models.js`, its springs in `reckoning-move.js`, questions from `reckoning-questions.js`. It is the endless run only, composed of segments (`reckoning-biomes.js`: generated from a biome, partly generated with `?`/`~` slots, or pre-made; the engine's `compose()` picks by biome, a rising `difficultyAt`, variety and a transition). The twenty ideas in `reckoning-sections.js` are the Playtest list: all open, no stars (`tests/courier-sections.mjs` wins each with a bot; `tests/courier-run.mjs` soaks each biome). It sits in Idle (4 November 2026: in Knowledge for a day, put back by the split review as a swipe runner, not a knowledge game): played on reflex, a question now and then. The built-in models are made in code (no model files); one world. It was called Reckoning until 3 November 2026: the name is now Courier on screen only, and ids, filenames, tests and the `reckoning:` saved keys keep the old one so saved state survives.

## The wall run
Korbi's words (4 November 2026) read as: a wall run resets the jump; most walls give no more height, a taller wall or a wall opposite gives a second jump, and the swipe after it joins a wall higher up. ("Sometimes always" read as "sometimes also".) One state machine (`side()`, `up()`, `step()` in `reckoning-engine.js`; `tests/courier-walls.mjs` has a test for every row):

| From | Swipe or event | To |
|---|---|---|
| run | sideways towards a wall in the edge lane I am in | wall, feet at `WALL_Y` (2 m) |
| run, middle lane | sideways towards a wall in the edge lane, no foe to cut in it | wall, by way of the lane, in the one swipe (a foe in that lane to cut makes it the cut; the wall run is the next swipe) |
| air, middle lane | sideways towards the wall, feet under its top | wall, at the height I was |
| air (any jump) | towards a wall in my edge lane, feet under its top | wall, at the height I was (never above top - 0.5); the jump is mine again |
| air, above the top | towards the wall | a lane change attempt; no wall (an ordinary wall, `WALL_TOP` 3.2 m, gives no height) |
| wall | up | air, `WALLJUMP_V`, and `kick` is set |
| air with `kick`, in the lane across | sideways towards a wall on the other side | wall there, at my height: chains upwards, `WALLJUMP_H` (2.27 m) a jump, to the wall's `top` |
| air with `kick`, same side | towards the wall | wall again, higher, if the wall's `top` allows (a taller wall) |
| wall | away from it, a foe in reach that way (a gull, a balloon, a crab below; `sideTarget`, the mid-air swipe's own test) | the aerial cut it would be in the air: lunge, kill, chain bounce, jump back; `wallleave` is still said first |
| wall | away from it, nothing in reach | air in the wall's lane, no `kick` |
| wall | towards it | nothing, and nothing is kept |
| wall | the wall's end | air, `wallend`; falls onto what is below (a deck, the planks, the water) |

A wall's `top` is its height (`WALL_TOP` when it has none); the ship's side stands 8.5 m over water, so it takes two jumps and two crossings.

## A lunging foe I meet
- 4 November 2026, Korbi, "should a roll in a lunging foe's path also knock it back, as a swipe does?": "yes; rolling or jump attack -> knocks straight back; swipe left -> knocks back and left; swipe right vice versa."

A foe that has launched (`ph` 3) and that I meet does not pass through me: it dies (`kill`, `how: "clash"`, `dir` the knock's; in the air an aerial kill, with the chain and the jump back), a bruiser loses the attack and is thrown back wounded (`stagger`), and I am not hurt (`clashDir()`, `clash()`). I meet it by:
- a **sideways swipe** that moved me (a lane change, a cut, a cut moved on) at most `CLASH_T` (0.3 s) ago, towards the lane the foe came from or any lane beyond it: knocked back and to that side (`dir` -1 left, +1 right);
- a **roll** under way on the ground (a landing roll too): knocked straight back (`dir` 0). Changed on 4 November: a roll used to be no dodge and no hit against a lunge from the side; now a ground foe lunging into my lane while I roll dies. A foe lunging down my own lane was already cut by the roll. A gull's dive is still gone under, not cut;
- a **jump attack**, an up swipe that jumped (ground, coyote or air jump) at most `CLASH_T` ago, against any launched foe whose lunge reaches me, gull or crab: straight back. It meets the foe over its head, so a jump that used to clear a lunge by a hair now kills it.
A swipe away is a dodge as before. Where a cut is possible the ordinary side cut still happens first (the clash is only the collision that is left, a late swipe); an old save has no `hero.swipe`/`jumpT` and has no clash.

The rules keep no position for a knocked foe (the view flies it: `knock()` in `reckoning-3d.js`, straight on down the quay or to its side). A foe knocked sideways is dead and lands nowhere near me. The one bonus: a ground foe standing in the lane it flies on to (the lane beyond its own, in the swipe's direction; its own lane when straight back), within `KNOCK_Z` (7 m) ahead of it, is knocked down too (50 points; a bruiser staggers), as `kill` with `knocked: true`.

## Traps
- The world bend (`curved()` in `reckoning-3d.js`) moves vertices only. A long box has eight, so it is bent as one straight chord while the little planks on it follow the true curve and ride up out of it: the stone showed between them as pale slabs, odd at the sides ("has always been like this"). Every long thing (the quay's stone, the water, decks, walls) is built with a vertex a metre or so along its length (`longBox`); a plank is cut to the stretch it lies on, or it hangs over a pit's lip. The darker plank is darker than the stone by multiplication, not mixed towards the ink (pale at night).
- A gull dives only while I am on the ground and not flying at something; a roll goes under it (it is not cut), and `rollRec` is the recovery after the last roll of a chain (a down in it waits its `BUFFER`, so a longer recovery eats the swipe).
- Swipes at a foe have no earliest time any more (a lane change is as quick as the cut), and a foe in `threat` (hitting me, `f.threat` the time it began) is still a target until `LATE_WIN` is out. Barrels and balloons are inert and hit at once.
- Swipe windows are in seconds to contact, not metres, so they are the same at 10 m/s and at 16. A foe's `aim` is fixed at its launch.
- The world is drawn in a group mirrored in x (`world`), and the camera gets the mirrored x. Lane +1 is the hero's right.
- The hero's lane x is a critically damped spring (`LANE_OMEGA`), so `h.x` is mid-arc during a change and `h.vx` carries its speed; a save from before has no `vx` (read it `|| 0`). A cut's y is eased; its x is the same spring.
- An up swiped in the air with nothing to cut is kept until the landing, up to `LAND_BUFFER`; any other swipe with nothing to work on waits `BUFFER`.
- Everything that is only feel (camera, lean, squash, cloth, dust, streaks, the pose carried over from the one before) is in the view and `reckoning-move.js`, which the rules never read; the page hands the view `lead`, the time the rules have not run yet, and the hero is drawn carried on by it. Tests play the rules alone.
- The hero's pose is numbers on the named joints (`J`, `poseHero`); the names are what a glTF's joints must match. On a wall he leans away and is drawn 0.5 m out from its face, or he is hidden inside the box.
- Models (`reckoning-models.js`): a joint is a Group with one merged mesh, coloured per vertex by palette slot (`retheme()` repaints every geometry; `curved()` is passed in, so the world bend stays on every material). The hero's joints keep their names and the pivots sit at real joints (the head turns at the neck, not the skull's middle); a glTF replacing him must name its joints the same. A lathe profile must run up the outside of the shape or its faces point inward (`band()` is a ring that does). Foes face +z and the page turns them half round, so the side the camera sees of a foe is its +z, of a thing its -z.
- A gull's beak points at the hero because foes are turned half round to face him. Sizes follow the Docs page "Reckoning: models and assets" (crab at most 1.5 m across, a gull's wings 1.6 m, only a question's gulls carry a banner), so a model replacing a box changes nothing the rules hold.
- `vendor/three-run.js` is its own esbuild bundle, not Harbour's: it has no `IcosahedronGeometry` and much else; check what it exports before using a class.
- Down in a jump with nothing under is known as a roll by its landing speed (a dive starts at 24 m/s, no fall of its own gets near), so nothing is remembered and a bonk, a lunge or the water cancels it.
- The slam never answers a question (a gull is in the air, the shockwave is on the ground). A wrong answer costs two hearts, one for the answer and one for the water.
- The swipe log is in the tab's `sessionStorage` (`reckoning:log`), not under the synced keys. `reckoning:miss` is kept with the entities for the pile but nothing reads it yet (`pile.js` is shared).
- A section's quay is made whole at its start (`ensure` does nothing when `S.sec`), from `S.rng` seeded by the section's id, so it is the same every run; only the questions are dealt afresh (`qrng`). A kept run whose section has since changed (`S.sig`) or is gone is dropped by `valid`; an endless run from before sections is given the fields it lacks.
- Writing a section: no thing to pick up in the row of a crab beside its lane (the crab is cut as it comes and the cut carries the hero off); a row of sluices has all three beats; keep 40 to 60 m before a `!gate`, `!chain` or `!wall` to crabs and rest only (the test's old bot plays those pieces and knows nothing of tokens or barrels); a bruiser needs 12 m after it. A section the bot cannot win at par does not get in.
- A class with `-go` in its name gets Office's button lettering (`[class*="-go"]` in `themes.css`): the aim under the hearts is `rk-aim` for that.
- Kept under `reckoning:`: `run`, `best` (the endless run's; also `courier:best`), `stats`, `miss`; `courier:coins`, `courier:amber` (banked when a run ends). An old `camp` key is ignored. A partner watching reads `run` and draws it without a clock.

- Sound (`reckoning-sound.js`, all synthesised; Sound and Haptics under `reckoning:sound`, `reckoning:haptics`): a new engine event type must go in its `EVENTS` or `SILENT` (`tests/courier-sound.mjs` fails otherwise); the footsteps and the bed run only while `setLive` is true (running, not paused, not over, tab visible), and a watching frame never calls `cue`.
- Barrels and balloons are foes (`KINDS`, `inert`): the attacks that cut a crab break them, nothing makes them look or fly at me. A barrel run into costs a heart and breaks; a balloon run into costs only the chain. Coins come out of a break (`BREAK_COINS`). The old `b` obstacle is gone from new quays (a kept run's is still handled).
- Coins have a random stream of their own (`crng`), so adding or moving them never moves a foe; `sprinkle` runs after a stretch is made. A trail never goes through a crab's lane or the one beside it, so the coins are the safe line.
- A sluice's lamp, ring/cross and leaf all come from `tide()`, the same function `solid()` uses: closing is still open, opening is still shut (the warning is the last `TIDE_WARN` seconds). A bridge's colour (red 0, blue 1) is its `flip`; the lever lowers the bridges that are up now.
- Ground above the quay is `W.decks` (lanes l0 to l1, height y, a `ramp` rising over its first metres, a `down` falling over its last; none means a sheer edge). Foes, coins and obstacles take `gy`, the ground under them, when they are made, so decks must be laid before the things on them; every test of "on the ground" is relative to `gy` (`gyOf(f)`, `h.gy`). A deck's sheer front run or jumped into more than 0.6 m too low costs a heart and puts me on it (`g > 0.5`: the far lip of a pit is not a deck). A drop off a deck is free. A pad (`W.pads`) throws me `PAD_V` (4.3 m) up and gives the jump back; the bot keeps off decks and pads (`freeLane`), so the soaks prove only that they can be avoided; `tests/courier-walls.mjs` plays them.
- The camera follows the ground level he stands on (a deck, a ramp, a wall) fully and the height of a jump in part, nearly all of it past a plain jump's (`CameraRig`, `reckoning-move.js`; `ground` is `h.gy`, or his own height on a wall, held after a wall jump until he lands), and the hero's shadow and a line down to it are drawn at `h.gy`, so height over a deck or water can be read. Only a phone can say whether it is enough.
