# Settings
Menu (No. button) → Settings. They apply from the next board.
- Difficulty: Easy, Mixed or Hard.
- Topics: a line saying where the topics stand and a link to the control, which is Settings' (README, The knowledge base). Country topics (19, each answering to the subject of the same name): Film, TV & games covers films, series, video and board games; Craft & style covers textiles and weaving, tailoring and menswear, shoes and watches. The commodity topics (Origins, Production, Uses, Specs & science, Markets, Trade & logistics, Policy, Companies, History, Culture & language) answer to the subjects Geography, Trade, Science, Finance and so on, and still shape the games that deal commodities. Money & economy is what an informed newspaper reader knows (currencies, central banks, household-name banks and indices, famous crises, sovereign funds); Finance is what someone working in markets knows (benchmarks and crude grades, bond markets, trading jargon, rogue traders and blow-ups). Benchmarks also carry their sector topic, so TTF comes up under Energy as well.
- Countries from: switch whole regions on or off (Europe, Middle East & N. Africa, Sub-Saharan Africa, Asia & Pacific, the Americas).

If the settings are too narrow to build a board, that board falls back to Balanced and says so.
