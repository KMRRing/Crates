# Slipway
A puzzle in 3D (`slipway.html`, `.js`, `.css`). A part of a ship's cog lies as a ghost in a block of timber; free it with a few tools, each used from the right side, in the right order and along the right grain, within a budget. Rules: `slipway-puzzle.js` (over the workshop `slipway-carve.js`); parts: `slipway-build.js` (features), `slipway-parts.js` and `slipway-levels.js` (31 in six chapters); generator `slipway-gen.js` (Random, Today's); words `slipway-tools.js`; bench `slipway-3d.js`; sounds `slipway-sound.js`. Eight tools (saw, back saw "kerf", chisel, plane, spokeshave, adze, drill, gouge), a par per part proved by exhaustive search, stars by tools used, a hint naming the next tool and the view, undo, and a ghost of what a tool would take before it takes it.

## Traps
- The flat bench (`slipway-2d.js`; 2D mode, no WebGL, or Menu, View, kept in `slipway:opts.look` only when picked) is the same camera as the 3D one (a perspective 233 cells away), so its `ray`, `eye` and `forward` give the saw the same plane and a tap the same hit; the named views are its plan, front and end. It must keep answering the calls `slipway.js` makes of `bench`: add one to both when the page starts calling a new one.
- A saw is the plane through the eye and the two ends of the drawn line, so the view it is drawn from is the puzzle. The camera is 233 cells away (near-orthographic), so a face seen edge-on is a line. The named views are exact (`VIEWS`; orbiting snaps within 3°). The Corner view is edge-on to some planes by coincidence: `tests/slipway-puzzle.mjs` draws every level's saws from it and fails if one lands, so a new slope must not be edge-on from there.
- The part's line has a pencil band: cells within 0.5 of it are neither waste nor part. It is why a wall that borders a chiselled pocket measures 0.5 thicker than it is (the partner's hole sits 0.7 from the mortise, not 1.2), and why the back saw counts the band as wood to score.
- A wall that thin splits only while the wood beside it is gone: a bore or a dish is safe while waste backs it. That is the rule behind "bore before you trim" and "scoop before you ease the edge". The block's own boundary is air, so a hole needs 1.8 cells of wood to the block's edge from the start.
- The drill's far face must be square too (`blowout`): the block rests on the bench, so a hole's exit is checked on the bench side as well.
- The plane, spokeshave and adze take a hole's or a dish's waste when their footprint crosses it (they only know "waste"), so a stroke over an unbored hole is not a mistake, only wasteful.
- The chisel is released by the kerfs along the grain only; the walls across it need none. A pocket that runs out at an end of the part has no wall there (`open` in `pocket`): its carve box is extended past the end, or the last cells read as part wood.
- The search proves a par only over the library a level declares (the saw on each face, strokes, chops, bores, dishes). A new feature must declare every use a player could try that is not already there, or its par is a claim, not a proof. `tests/slipway-search.mjs` runs a rotating slice each push and everything with `SLIPWAY_ALL=1`; run it whole before changing a rule or the generator.
- Two slopes meeting over a cap saw the cap off between them (the ridge is under 0.7 cells): `generate` drops the cap then, and the stem knee's slopes are steep for the same reason.
- A knot must be on the timber's surface for the shader to paint it, and the sphere it paints is the one the plane, spokeshave and drill stop on.
- `vendor/three.js` is a cut-down bundle: no `LineLoop`, `Points`, `PlaneGeometry` or orthographic camera (the saw's guide is `LineSegments`, chips are small boxes).
- Saved: `slipway:carve` (the part in hand, version 3: `run` is `{ kind: level|random|daily, at | seed, reach }`, the block run-length coded, its kerfs; a version 2 save is read as its part among the first seven), `slipway:parts` (`{ id: { s: stars, u: tools used } }`, kept by part id from the first puzzle), `slipway:best` (the stars together), `slipway:rand`, `slipway:daily` (`{ day: stars }`), `slipway:mode`, `slipway:opts`. Saves of the earlier carving game are ignored, never read as stars.
