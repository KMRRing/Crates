# Asterism
Star Battle (4 November 2026, named per the Nikoli rule in `classics.md`; "star battle" is a search keyword only). N×N board in N regions, K stars (1, or 2 on 8 to 10) in every row, column and region, no two touching even diagonally. One answer per puzzle, proven by a plain row-by-row search (`answers`); level is the hardest step the solver needs: Easy (a star's surroundings, full groups, just enough room), Medium (a group's few ways, a star that leaves a group no room, a region held to a line), Hard (counting across two lines, a trial within 4 rounds), Expert (three lines, a longer trial).

- Tap cycles empty, dot, star; hold or right-click makes a star; a drag dots, held to the row or column it began on (4 November 2026, Korbi: a wandering finger left stray marks; `axisStroke`, axis picked once, lift to turn). Only stars count; dots are notes.
- Two stars a row has no Easy (none exists: they need Medium steps from the first move) and 5×5 has no Expert; `levelOn`/the line say so. Two stars start at 8×8 (7×7 and smaller made no unique puzzle).
- Times are lowest-first in `asterism:times`, not `:best` (the games screen reads `:best` as highest-is-best); the tile shows today's time.
- Space view (4 November 2026, Korbi: "Asterism has no numbers, so it is the obvious candidate for a really beautiful 3D, luminescent, floating-in-space visualisation"): the default; the flat grid stays a menu choice and is where a slow device lands (`asterism-space.js`, a 2D canvas, no WebGL). A tap is picked through the projection with the glass taken half way up a star's hover.
- Space, later that day (Korbi): the board stays where it is turned, no ease-back (menu: Reset the view); the look is dark and restrained, not neon (muted regions told apart by value, a faint pattern and the brass rule; stars warm white; conflicts dull oxblood; the solve a slow warming, no flash); a one-finger drag dots along its axis as on the flat grid, and the board turns with two fingers, the Turn button, shift, or a drag begun off the pane.
- Shapes (4 November 2026, Korbi asked whether it can be 3D like Mines): Cube (4 a side: a line is a belt, the ring round the cube at one layer; every cell is on two) and Hexagons (side 4 to 7: the rows in three directions), one star in every line and region, none touching, drawn in space only (`asterism-solid.js`, `frameMesh`); the daily stays flat. The star count stays 1 and the cube stays 4: a bigger cube cannot be made unique with regions worth playing (6 and 8 a side left regions of two or three cells, or one of half the board, to separate thousands of arrangements), and two stars a belt gives 300000+ arrangements on a 4.
- Cube puzzles and the larger hexagons (5 to 7) are made ahead (`asterism-bank.js`, with one answer and a level each; grading them takes seconds to minutes) and dealt by seed in one of the shape's symmetries (48, 12). A cube has no Easy, nor does a hexagon of 5 or more; the line says so. A cube's regions are lopsided (one often holds half of it): that is what makes it unique.
- Not shipped: the rhombicuboctahedron (a line needs straight rings through its cells: its 8 triangles lie on no belt and its 12 edge squares on one, so the "one star in every line" rule has nothing to grip) and the football (hexagon rows break at each of its 12 pentagons; regions alone give thousands of answers).
- Not done: a pair or party mode, a computer rival.
