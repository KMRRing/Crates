# Drift
The ninth idle game (October 2026; README, Idle): `drift.html`, `drift.js`, `drift.css`; the rules are `drift-engine.js` (pure, read its header first), the quays `drift-shapes.js`, the levels `drift-levels.js`, the simple players `drift-bots.js` (tests and tuning only, the page never loads it); the 3D board is `drift-3d.js` and its sounds `drift-sound.js`. Crates of four goods (salt, herring, furs, amber; the engine knows them as 0-3) lie on a quay, a mask on a grid (a hull, a pier, a basin, a figure of eight, a lagoon, the open square); each turn you lay the next crate on any empty square, then the wind blows and every crate slides downwind to the edge of its stretch of quay, keeping its order, and three or more alike in a line ship. It is 2048 turned round: the forecast chooses the way and you lay the tile. The queue shows five turns ahead, each with its crate, wind and weather (squall, rogue wave, second gust, fog, rock, a moored crate, the harbour master's sealed crate). Only the crate to lay is shown beforehand (a ghost of it on the square you press); the wind's result is seen only as it plays out. **Voyage** is sixteen levels of orders in eight sections, one new event to a section; **Open sail** is the endless game for a score on a quay dealt at random.

## Traps
- There is no preview of a turn's result and must not be: the page calls `lay()` once, on let go, and the forecast's strays, rock and wave are exactly what `lay()` then plays.
- The flat and the 3D board answer the same calls (`draw`, `place`, `arrive`, `shove`, `slide`, `ship`, `popup`, `finish`, `cell`); a tap during a turn calls `finish()` and the rest of it is drawn at once. The 3D board draws only while a tween runs and, at about fifteen frames a second, the sea while the page is showing and not paused; `view.step(ms)` is for looking at a turn mid-motion.
- A saved v1 game (the square field of seven) is made into an Open sail game on the open quay by `upgrade`; its best (`drift:best` was a number) is the open quay's, and the key is now bests by quay. `drift:seen` was the old first-turns hint, a boolean; the section events seen are `drift:events`.
- The bots never use a sealed crate or a wave to their advantage, so the planner's win rate understates a person's on the levels with them (8-1 most); the levels are tuned so random wins at most about a third and the planner about half to nine tenths.
- A class `.crate` is Crates' in `style.css`: every class here is `dr-` or inside an `svg`.
