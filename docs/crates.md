# Crates
Sixteen words, four hidden crates. Group the four words that belong together, then name the country behind them. The menu's Mode choice (Countries | Classic) is the other half of the game: Classic is Lots, sixteen words in four lots with a link to see (README, Lots), a page of its own under Crates' name and logo.

Crates and Lots (2 November 2026; Korbi: "crates should be combined with lots. lots should simply be a subgroup of crates, similar to crosswords. commodities mode for crates should go, essentially replaced by the classic"). Lots lost its tile and is reached from Crates' header, as Puzzle rush is from Chess's: two pages under one name, not one engine. Crates deals countries only: the commodity boards went from the header, the settings, the daily and the games screen's Crates, because Classic's markets and things-with lots are where that sort of link lives now. The commodity answers and their clues stay in the bank (Punt, Crossword, Nexus and the others deal from them, and the codes of boards already shared still open: an old `?cat=commodities` or `?cat=mixed` link is countries now, a saved run or a room that had another pool is read as countries from its next board, and a commodity board already begun is finished). What it cost: Lots' daily no longer has a tile to show it (it still counts: `lots:daily`, `lots:best`), and Random, which deals a game per tile, no longer deals Lots.

Topics are Settings' (README, The knowledge base): Crates keeps no topics of its own. Each board is dealt from Balanced with the topics whose subject is off taken out, so all subjects on is exactly Balanced and a seed deals what it always dealt; if the subjects on leave no country topic, every topic is dealt, as the control says. Balanced has every topic in, and more of each except the four specialist ones (watches, skiing, the art market, etiquette), which are in once. The menu's Content part says where the topics stand and links to the control.

Held sideways (October 2026; README, Bigger screens), the board stands on the left, as large as the height lets four rows of tiles be and centred in it, and the strip, what's happening, your guesses so far and the buttons stand beside it, the buttons at the bottom where a thumb is.

One board with Classic (3 November 2026; Korbi: "the formatting is different between countreis & classic: both should be the same formatting, and centered", and the wall and the computer rival, which he found made little sense, are gone; see Lots): the page is `#app.cboard` and `crates-board.js` draws it, as Lots does, from `crates-board.css`. A crate found is a bar in the board's rows, as a lot is, so nothing grows when one is tapped: its clues and red herrings open in a sheet. The strip over the board holds the mistakes and clues left, the line under it what to do, what a guess came to or the clue on show, and the result (Share, Next board) takes the buttons' place. style.css still holds Crates' old rules for the grid, tiles and crates; this page no longer uses them.

One type size on the tiles (3 November 2026; Korbi: "make the size of the font for crates consistent, or maybe just between 2 options, the way it is right now with every crate has a different size font is quite irritating"): every tile is one size (`--t` times .14, so it grows with the screen), and a run of letters longer than nine is broken with soft hyphens (`crates-tile.js`), never shrunk. The board is shared with Lots, which gets the same.
