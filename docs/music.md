# Music

A course in music theory and ear training: 24 lessons in eight chapters (sound and the keyboard, reading music, intervals, scales and keys, chords, rhythm, ear training, a little piece), each with an instrument (keyboard, staff, circle of fifths, chord and progression builders, metronome with a tap pad, ear trainer). `music.html` is the door; the lessons (`music-lessons.js`) run on the shared course sheet (`course.js`) with their own stage (`music-stage.js`). All sound is synthesised (Web Audio, `music-audio.js`), so nothing is recorded or licensed; every table is computed in `music-theory.js`. Progress is the sheet's `music:course`; `music:lessons` is `{ done, of }` for the tile; `music:calib` is the tap offset in ms; `music:pile` is the ear review pile (`music-pile.js`, a small Leitner ladder of its own; Ledger's and Punt's piles are not touched).

It trains hearing, which no game here does. Needs care on iOS: audio starts only after a tap, and latency must be checked on a real iPhone."

## Traps

- **iOS starts audio only inside a tap.** `audio.unlock()` makes and resumes the context and plays a silent sample; it is called from every pointerdown, pointerup, touchend and click on the stage and from the page's buttons. Until the state is "running" the stage shows an "Enable sound" band. Never make an `AudioContext` at load.
- **The silent switch.** On iOS before 17, Web Audio is muted by the side switch; `unlock()` sets `navigator.audioSession.type = "playback"` where it exists (Safari 17+) and otherwise plays a silent looping clip made in memory (an `<audio>` element on a Blob, no file). The first lesson tells the reader. Only an iPhone can confirm this works; the silent clip may show in the lock-screen media controls.
- **Latency.** Rhythm runs on the audio clock (`createLoop`, a look-ahead scheduler), never on `setTimeout` timing. A tap's `event.timeStamp` is put on that clock with `getOutputTimestamp` (Safari lacks it: the fallback pairs the clocks at the tap). The rest, the device's and the reader's own delay, is measured by the calibration lesson (median of tap minus click) and subtracted when grading. `baseLatency` and `outputLatency` are only shown.
- **The course sheet is a `pauses` dialog**, so the generic pause would silence the course while it is open. `music.js` pauses for every other pauser (menu, games screen, confirmations) and a hidden page, not for the sheet.
- **Accessibility.** Every ear exercise lights dots in time with the sound (no pitch shown) and has Skip; a skipped drill counts as done at no penalty, so the course can be finished without hearing. Rhythm tasks need the audio clock and have Skip too. A watching frame makes no sound (`createAudio({ frame })`) and says so.
- **The voice cap is 24**; the oldest voice is let go (faded in 10 ms). One oscillator a note (a periodic wave), so a chord is cheap.
- **Spelling is by letter**: a scale uses each letter once (B♭, not A♯, in F major); intervals are spelled by counting letters. The keyboard's labels are sharps unless a step says `flats`.
