# Coming soon

Games on the list that aren't built (31 October 2026, Korbi: "Bridges, masyu, nurikabe, slitherlink, rack, go, pegs, meld, French tarot if not already there… I want all of these but have used 84% of weekly limit… the rest just get a tile placeholder with todo for now"). Each has a tile on the games screen, in the section it will live in, with a logo in each house's drawing (`soon-logos.js`). The tile opens `soon.html?game=<id>` (one page for all of them, `soon.js` and `soon.css`), which names the game, says in a sentence what it will be, and lists what's left to do, with where the risk is: those notes are `soon-data.js`, the plan in one place. Nothing is kept, nothing plays, and nothing syncs. Built since: Bridges, Masyu, Nurikabe, Slitherlink, Rack, Pegs and Meld. Waiting: Go (on the choice of a computer opponent) and Tarot (on a bot for a game with bidding).

A placeholder is nothing else in the app: Random leaves it out ("not built yet", `LEFT_OUT`), Start a game over doesn't list it, and its best isn't shared with your partner. When a game is built, its entry leaves `soon-data.js`, its logos move into `apps.js` with the rest, its tile points at its own page, and it goes in Random's `ROUNDS` or `LEFT_OUT` with a reason; `tests/soon.mjs` fails while a tile and its entry disagree, and `tests/random.mjs` while a tile is in neither list. A tile's name is a working name (a string in `apps.js` and `soon-data.js`); its id is what saves and Random's lists use, so a rename costs nothing.

The falling-blocks game (Cargo) was cut on 3 November 2026 (README). Flank and Crown are our own searches on `seats.js`: the strong free engines for them (Edax, Scan) are GPL (a licence that obliges you to publish the source of the whole program).

On chess, Korbi's call: Gambit keeps its engine only if one under a permissive licence will do (its Stockfish is GPL v3, in `vendor/`; River's engine is its own now); the worst case is puzzles only, so nothing GPL has to be in the app. Which permissive engine is the open question (docs/licences-images-and-engines.md).
