# Coming soon

Games on the list that aren't built (31 October 2026, Korbi: "Bridges, masyu, nurikabe, slitherlink, rack, go, pegs, meld, French tarot if not already there… I want all of these but have used 84% of weekly limit… the rest just get a tile placeholder with todo for now"). Each has a tile on the games screen, in the section it will live in, with a logo in each house's drawing (`soon-logos.js`). The tile opens `soon.html?game=<id>` (one page for all of them, `soon.js` and `soon.css`), which names the game, says in a sentence what it will be, and lists what's left to do, with where the risk is: those notes are `soon-data.js`, the plan in one place. Nothing is kept, nothing plays, and nothing syncs. Built since: Bridges, Masyu, Nurikabe, Slitherlink, Rack, Pegs and Meld. Waiting: Stone (on the choice of a computer opponent) and Tarot (on a bot for a game with bidding).

A placeholder is nothing else in the app: Random leaves it out ("not built yet", `LEFT_OUT`), Start a game over doesn't list it, and its best isn't shared with your partner. When a game is built, its entry leaves `soon-data.js`, its logos move into `apps.js` with the rest, its tile points at its own page, and it goes in Random's `ROUNDS` or `LEFT_OUT` with a reason; `tests/soon.mjs` fails while a tile and its entry disagree, and `tests/random.mjs` while a tile is in neither list. A tile's name is a working name (a string in `apps.js` and `soon-data.js`); its id is what saves and Random's lists use, so a rename costs nothing.

**Not a tile yet: unit chains** (Korbi, 2 November 2026: "one more fast arithmetic game that essentially is just unit conversions", to be recorded and not built). Beside Spot in Puzzles: the computer picks a start figure and a chain of operations, each unit followed by the next step, and you type the answer as fast as you can. Korbi's examples are all commodities, in the order a trader does them: a flow in thousand barrels a day; how many tonnes a month; 5% of that; a saving of 80% against a baseline of 94 gCO2e/MJ and how many tonnes of CO2e a month that saves; at 120 GBP a tonne, how many pounds a month; the same in dollars a year. Long chains of mental arithmetic, not necessarily logical ones, each step the next unit's operation. What it needs: a pool of figures and conversions with their factors (barrels to tonnes by product, energy content, emission factors, exchange rates fixed at the start so the answer is exact), chains of three to six steps that end on a figure a person can type (rounded to a stated place, a tolerance, or integers by construction), a timer and a streak, and a plain-language pool (litres to gallons, miles to kilometres, recipes) so the public app has a way in that is not oil. The data lives in `kb/units.js`, which already holds the units.
