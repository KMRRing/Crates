# Yield
Seven letters on a tray: make words of four letters or more from them, every word with the key letter (the keyed navy plate) in it, any letter as often as you like. Four letters score a point, a longer word a point a letter, and a full set (a word using all seven) seven more; every puzzle has at least one. Ten ranks from Dry hole to Last drop, each a share of the puzzle's points (Supergiant, 70%, is the one to aim for; Last drop is every word). The name is the refinery's: yield is how much of each product one crude gives, here how many words seven letters give. The rules, the puzzles, the levels, the daily and the computer player are `yield-engine.js`'s, pure and tested in Node; the page is `yield.js`.

**4 November 2026 (Korbi): redesign.** The first Yield looked like another newspaper's word game (a hive of hexagons with a yellow centre, the genre's own term for the all-letters word, rank tiers cut to match its percentages), and that newspaper enforces its game names and look. So: no hexagon hive and no yellow centre; the letters sit in a brass-framed tray, the key letter on its own navy plate (a tall bar at the left, or a long one across the top in a tall box; `layoutOf` in `yield.js`), the other six in a rack of two rows; the full set is "Full set"; the ranks are a well's career (Dry hole, Spudded, Seeping, Shows, Flowing, Producing, Plateau, Gusher, Supergiant, Last drop); the accent is cobalt. The play is unchanged. Saved ids are unchanged on purpose: boards `hive` and `wheel` (shown as Tray and Plate), `centre` in a puzzle (the key letter), the `yield:` keys.

Traps.
- Supergiant stays at 70% (`RANKS[MASTER]`): the levels' depth grading and the dailies' walk in `yield-words.js` are measured at it. Moving it needs the walk rebuilt (`tests/yield.mjs` fails until it matches the engine).
- The words are `yield-words.js` (SCOWL by Zipf frequency from wordfreq, CC BY-SA 4.0; never an S, a one-side-of-the-Atlantic spelling or a blocked word), written by `tools/yield-words.mjs`. A word typed that doesn't count is told apart (rarer than counted, one side's spelling, not in the list); a slur is never called a word.
- A puzzle is never mostly one ending (-ing 30%, -ed 45% at most). Light, Medium and Heavy are depth, how far down the vocabulary Supergiant needs you to reach, not size.
- Today's puzzle is a Medium tray from the fixed walk (`today()`), kept apart from a random one (`yield:day`, `yield:run`, `yield:last`); points go to the games screen as each word is found (`yield:daily`).
- A team's phones each write only their own words, and the database keeps no empty arrays or objects; the team's yield is everyone's together (`teamFound`). Each player has a key letter of their own and holds the next player's chart.
- Keep every size in rem; the plate is an SVG drawn to a viewBox, so its text stays in px.
