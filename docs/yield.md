# Fathom (id `yield`)
Seven letters on a tray: make words of four letters or more from them, every word with the key letter (the keyed navy plate) in it, any letter as often as you like. Four letters score a point, a longer word a point a letter, and a full set (a word using all seven) seven more; every puzzle has at least one. Ten ranks from Aground to Blue water, each a share of the puzzle's points (Deep sea, 70%, is the one to aim for; Blue water is every word). The name is a leadsman's: a fathom is six feet, the unit of a sounding line; each word is a cast of the lead and a longer word is deeper water. The rules, the puzzles, the levels, the daily and the computer player are `yield-engine.js`'s, pure and tested in Node; the page is `yield.js`.

Renamed Fathom (id, files and `yield:` keys unchanged). The refinery's column, slate, cut names and sounds are gone; the twist is a hand lead's line, a vertical line beside the tray with the real marks (`MARKS` in `yield-engine.js`): leather two tails 2 fathoms, three tails 3, white calico 5, red bunting 7, leather with a hole 10, blue serge 13, then calico 15, bunting 17, two knots 20. The line pays out with the points: each rank sits at one mark, in order, and between two ranks the depth is in proportion (`lineDepth`). A word is a cast: its length in letters is the fathoms it sounds, and the leadsman calls it ("By the mark five" on a marked fathom, "By the deep four" on an unmarked one, "And a half six" when a letter is used twice, "No bottom!" for a full set). Words are listed by length (`GROUPS`). A chart of a harbour's approach fills with soundings as the line pays out (`yield-chart.js`). Sounds (`yield-sound.js`, off in the menu's Settings): lead splash, line running out, a bell for a mark. The logo is a sounding lead on its marked line over a wave (Punt's is a boat).

Traps.
- Deep sea stays at 70% (`RANKS[MASTER]`, once Supergiant): the levels' depth grading and the dailies' walk in `yield-words.js` are measured at it. Moving it needs the walk rebuilt (`tests/yield.mjs` fails until it matches the engine).
- The words are `yield-words.js` (SCOWL by Zipf frequency from wordfreq, CC BY-SA 4.0; never an S, a one-side-of-the-Atlantic spelling or a blocked word), written by `tools/yield-words.mjs`. A word typed that doesn't count is told apart (rarer than counted, one side's spelling, not in the list); a slur is never called a word.
- A puzzle is never mostly one ending (-ing 30%, -ed 45% at most). Light, Medium and Heavy are depth, how far down the vocabulary Deep sea needs you to reach, not size.
- Today's puzzle is a Medium tray from the fixed walk (`today()`), kept apart from a random one (`yield:day`, `yield:run`, `yield:last`); points go to the games screen as each word is found (`yield:daily`).
- A team's phones each write only their own words, and the database keeps no empty arrays or objects; the team's yield is everyone's together (`teamFound`). Each player has a key letter of their own and holds the next player's chart.
- Keep every size in rem; the plate is an SVG drawn to a viewBox, so its text stays in px.
