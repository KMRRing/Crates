# Wine course: facts to check

Written from memory, with no source consulted (4 November 2026). Each is stated in the course as below; the ones a reviewer should confirm first are marked **check**.

- **check** Named-grape minimum on a label: 85% in the EU and Australia, 75% in the USA (`wine-labels.js`, `place-grape`).
- **check** Champagne: non-vintage at least 15 months in all (12 on the lees), vintage at least three years; pressure about six atmospheres; dosage bands Brut Nature 0 to 3 g/l, Extra Brut 0 to 6, Brut up to 12, Extra Dry 12 to 17, Sec 17 to 32 (`wine-service.js`, `champagne`).
- **check** Châteauneuf-du-Pape "up to thirteen grape varieties" (some count eighteen with colour variants).
- **check** Brunello di Montalcino "at least five years of ageing"; Champagne has 17 Grand Cru villages; Beaujolais has ten named crus.
- **check** Serving temperatures and chilling times (6 to 8, 8 to 10, 10 to 14, 15 to 18 °C; about three hours in a fridge, 20 to 30 minutes in iced water) are rules of thumb, not standards. Storage "about 10 to 15 °C, heat above about 25 °C cooks it" likewise. Restaurant mark-up "often two or three times" is a rule of thumb.
- Chaptalisation "banned in much of southern Europe" (allowed in the cooler EU zones, with limits).
- Spain "has the largest vineyard area in the world" (also bank WN-159): OIV figures put Spain first, but China and France are close and the order changes with the year.
- South Africa: Chenin Blanc the most planted grape; Pinotage a 1925 cross of Pinot Noir and Cinsault.
- Judgment of Paris 1976: worded as "put Californian wines ahead of top French ones" (the winners were a 1973 Chardonnay and a 1973 Cabernet).
- 1855: "ranked by the prices the wines fetched"; Mouton Rothschild the only promotion (1973).
- Hong Kong dropped wine duty in 2008; Lafite engraved a Chinese eight on its 2008 bottles; Kurniawan convicted in 2013; Robert Parker's name made on the 1982 Bordeaux (WN-154).
- EU renewable-energy rules list grape marc and wine lees as advanced feedstocks (WN-160, RED Annex IX Part A): confirm the letter of the entry before quoting it.
- Not used because not verified: bank WN-101 (Satigny the largest Swiss wine commune) and WN-102 (Swiss exports 1 to 2%). Other bank questions were used as written.
- Italian "Superiore" glossed as "a higher minimum alcohol" (some zones also require longer ageing).
