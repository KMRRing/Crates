# Puzzle rush
Eight games have a puzzle rush, and it has the same settings in every one, in the same words (October 2026, asked for when Rush moved into Gambit: `rush-core.js` has the rules of it, `rush-menu.js` the controls). Gambit's chess puzzles, River's Xiangqi, Hong's and Tribute's take the game's place on the page; Schafkopf's (Trumps), Gammon's, Banquet's and Bones' are a sheet over it (`rush-sheet.js`). Each opens from its game's menu as Puzzle rush (until then Schafkopf's was "Schafkopf puzzle rush" and Banquet's "Call it", and only the chess Rush and River's had a choice of clock or a today's run: the other six were five minutes).

A run is Random, or Today's: the same puzzles in the same order for everyone that day (a game may add kinds of its own after them: River's Patterns). Its clock is 3 min, each puzzle's first five seconds free as a chess clock's delay works (it waits, then runs, and never gains, so it never shows more than three minutes); 5 min; or Survival, no clock at all. Three misses end a run on any clock. Where the rush takes the game's place, the choices are in its menu while it's open (Start a run starts one), and the menu is the rush's: Start a run, the last run's results and Back to the game; the run and the clock; Reset my rating where the puzzles are rated (chess and Xiangqi: your rating moves Elo-style against each puzzle's, and a run serves puzzles round it); your rating and your bests. On the sheet they're on its first screen, before Start and again after a run, as the same controls, with your best on the clock chosen. A rush opens on the kind and clock of the last run started.

Your best is kept for each clock, and for a game's own kind of run, whether the run was random or today's; today's runs are kept by the day. A best kept before the clocks were chosen was a five-minute run's, and counts as one; the chess Rush's today's bests ("three:daily") count for their clock. A game's About gives its rush's bests in a line ("Puzzle rush bests: 3 min 12, 5 min 15, Survival 30"); the rush's own menu gives a line for each clock with today's run beside it. A run's results are headed Time, Three misses or Every puzzle done.

How a game deals and judges its puzzles stays its own: a board's (chess, Xiangqi) are a line of moves, the clock running through them and an easy warm-up starting it; the others are positions with one clearly best play, where the verdict says what each play was worth and the clock stands while you read it (and while the sheet is closed or the app is away). The positions are dealt rising, four at each of ten ratings, those seen lately last (`runOrder` in `rush-core.js`; Hong and Tribute deal theirs alike); today's run takes today as its seed and leaves nothing out, so every phone gets the same. `tests/rush.mjs` checks the clocks and their grace, a run's clock of either kind, the bests and what was kept before them, today's runs, the words, the rating's steps, and that every game's rush uses all of it: the rushes on a page the shared menu, the sheet the same controls, no rush a clock of its own, and every game's menu the name Puzzle rush and its About the bests.
