# Ledger
4 November 2026, Korbi: "ledger should do a more in depth review of the user's progress across all applications, knowledge, revision, different strengths & weaknesses etc".

Ledger is the review of how you are doing, read from what the suite already keeps: every game's saved keys (bests, stars, lessons, dailies; `ledger-review.js` finds them by prefix and never writes them), the pile, the knowledge base's index (`kb-index.js`, fetched after the first draw, as Deck does) and the record Ledger keeps itself (`ledger:stats`, `ledger-log.js`). Held upright, a headline and five tabs, one card at a time scrolling in its own box; held sideways the cards stand side by side. A new player sees what will appear, and why it has not yet.
- **Headline:** the last whole ISO week against the week before: items learned, recall after a week or more away, games played, dailies kept, each with an arrow. Games played is a floor: a game counts for a week only if a daily, a pile answer or its latest save falls in it, since most games keep only their latest write.
- **Topics:** from the pile, by the subject of each item's entities (`about`): items held, learned, recall rate, items last missed in the last 30 days. Subjects with three or more items are ranked (smoothed recall, then the share learned); the three strongest and weakest are shown, the rest folded. "Review this" goes to `deck.html#topic=<label>`, which Deck does not yet read (its scope is only the device's own choice, `suite:deck`), so today it opens Deck.
- **Revision:** due now, how late, learned in 7 days, the six piles as one bar, recall by gap, and the streak of days with a review (the days Ledger notes as items come back, `ledger:stats.days`, and each item's latest answer).
- **Games:** by section of the games screen: played lately, bests with when the key was last written (`suite:meta`, so only with a solo code), stars, levels, lessons, dailies of the last 14 days as dots. Started and stalled (no sign for 14 days), and courses with lessons left, are called out. Settings keys are no sign of play.
- **Judgement:** Punt's calibration and Quote's markets, and a plain read per subject from the pile's Punt items ("you commit more than you know on Law"). Wording follows Punt's (`tests/no-gambling.mjs`): a venture, the share committed, the purse, a hold (never bet, stake, pot, pass, all-in, odds).
- **Next steps:** the weakest subject to review, the course with lessons left (else a stalled game), a daily not yet done today, each a link.

Recall: every item from the pile that comes back, in Deck or in a game, right or wrong, sorted by how long it was away (within the hour, two days, a week or two, a month, three months); away a week or more and still known is the measure that lasts. Confidence: every commitment in Punt, read backwards through Kelly's rule (committing a share f of the purse at a return of o times the share (2 for Punt's doubling), you act as if your chance were (1 + f(o − 1)) / o), plotted against how often those came off, a dot a tenth, the diagonal the ideal; the Brier score (the mean squared gap between that chance and what happened) sums it up (0 perfect, .250 always saying evens). Markets: in Quote, how often the truth lay inside your bid and ask, and the grades. The games report as they settle, to `ledger-log.js` (the pile on every item that comes back, Punt on every commitment, Quote on every market), which keeps weekly totals under `ledger:stats`, a few kilobytes however much you play, synced with the solo code like everything else. The record can be started afresh (Menu) without touching the piles. Tested in `tests/ledger.mjs` (the record) and `tests/ledger-review.mjs` (the review, on the synthetic profile in `tests/ledger-fake.js`).

Knowledge, read two ways: each Punt commitment also records the chance a pure guess had (one in the number of options; one in the ways to pick several right ones; none for a typed answer). A share's Kelly chance less that, over what's left (the standard correction for guessing), is the share of the answer the commitment claimed to know: a hold claims none, everything claims all, and at a fair price it's the share itself. Your hit rate corrected the same way is what you knew (right half the time on four options is knowing a third). Ledger sets the two side by side, says whether your shares rise with what you know, and counts only commitments made since Punt began recording the guess's chance.
