# Speed maths and Fuel maths

Two tiles on one engine (3 November 2026): **Speed maths** in Puzzles (`speedmaths.html`, a plain-language pool) and **Fuel maths** in the Appendix (`speedfuels.html`, a fuel desk's figures; its deck is listed for review in `docs/speedfuels.md`). Spot stays the other fast-arithmetic game (tap the right answer among fakes); this one is typed.

**Korbi's words.** 2 November 2026: "add a speed maths placeholder in puzzle". Two versions: "a generic mercantile one in Puzzles and a fuel-pricing one for his refining work", with "typed estimates accepted to one or two significant figures depending on the calculation". And: "one more fast arithmetic game that essentially is just unit conversions". His examples are all commodities, in the order a trader does them: a flow in thousand barrels a day; how many tonnes a month; 5% of that; a saving of 80% against a baseline of 94 gCO2e/MJ and how many tonnes of CO2e a month that saves; at 120 GBP a tonne, how many pounds a month; the same in dollars a year. Long chains of mental arithmetic, not necessarily logical ones, each step the next unit's operation. That chain is `flow-co2-dollars` in the fuels deck.

**The rule (`judge`, `speedmaths-core.js`).** A plain sum is exact. An estimate or chain asked to 1 or 2 significant figures is right when it is within 5% or 1% of the exact figure, or is exactly the exact figure rounded as asked. Both are needed: rounding alone has cliffs (14.9 and 15.1 round to 10 and 20 at one figure though they are 1% apart), and a band alone refuses the answer the words ask for when the exact figure starts with a 1 (to 1 s.f., 1.4 is 1, 29% out). A chain's band grows by half a band a step past the third, since honest shortcuts (7.5 barrels a tonne for 7.45) pile up. A slip of the decimal point is never close. `tests/speedmaths.mjs` sweeps every combination of every chain's figures: working it with its conversion factors rounded to two figures must be accepted, so a new chain's factors go in the pool's `constants`, or the test cannot see its shortcut.

**Real life only.** 4 November 2026, Korbi: "speed maths: make sure that all of the calculations are the kinds you'd encounter in real life. grow the possible scenarios a bit." No bare "37 × 8": every sprint sum is a situation with the same operation and the same numbers (`SCENARIOS` in `speedmaths-pool.js`: change from a note, a tip, VAT, a deposit, lots and tonnes), in a prompt of at most 40 characters (two lines at 390 px). Every estimate and chain (`DEFS`) states figures that occur (three digits at most; a conversion factor goes in `constants`). Reading a sentence costs about a second, so a standard sprint scores a little lower than before; the old best stands, since the sums and ranges are unchanged.

**What counts.** Only a standard sprint (every operation, range to 100) sets `speedmaths:best`, since a sprint of additions to 20 is no match for one of everything; other settings keep their own bests in `speedmaths:bests`. Fuel maths' `:best` is its chains. Today's is dealt from the date with none of your menu in it, and each pool has its own; it counts the moment the clock runs out. A race (Speed maths only) deals a sprint from the match's seed and runs on the table's clock, which the menu does not stop.

**Traps.** The figures of Fuel maths are in `speedfuels-data.js`, not `kb/units.js` (it was left alone: shared), with their sources and date at its head. Every figure a problem uses is written in the problem; none is assumed. The i-th sum is a pure function of the seed and i, so a saved run is picked up by its seed and index, and a changed generator makes a saved run deal different sums: bump `v` in `startRun` if one changes.
