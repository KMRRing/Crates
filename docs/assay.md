# Assay
Guess a hidden word. An assay tests what a sample is made of, and here the guess is weighed: letters weigh by their place in the alphabet (A the lightest), and each place of a guess reads right (◆, green), ▲ (the word's letter there comes later) or ▼ (earlier). A place narrows by halves, and a strip under the board says what each place may still be. The classic colour marks (in place, elsewhere, out) are the menu's other reading, Marks › Colours. Six guesses for five letters, seven for four. The rules, the words, the levels and the daily are `assay-engine.js`'s, the computer players `assay-solve.js`'s, the team's table `assay-table.js`'s, all pure; the page is `assay.js`.

**4 November 2026 (Korbi): the clone problem.** The New York Times enforces its game names and look (takedowns to hundreds of clones of its word game in 2024), and Assay was that game: its name in the docs and keywords, its colours, its square-emoji share grid. Rulings: no mention of it anywhere; a new share format; the classic 5-letter guess-the-word stays, but as a mode, not the default; a twist becomes the default. Three twists were weighed:
- Weighed (built): feedback is a reading per place, right / later / earlier in the alphabet, not colours. It keeps all three marks, so the engine, solvers, dodging word, boards, sprint and ladder serve both readings; a solver that guesses only words still possible needs 3.4 guesses weighed against 3.5 in colours (measured over 150 words), and Steady's level pars barely moved.
- Probe one place: each guess shows the mark of one chosen place only. Cheap to build, but it throws information away (judged, not measured) and reads as a handicap, not a new idea.
- Twin words: one guess, one set of marks for two hidden words. It is the multi-board mode with the hints merged, so it is frustrating more than clever (the engine's Boards mode already plays several words).
Weighed won: a different deduction (binary search per place, with a strip to hold it), on the same size of board, with a name that fits an assayer's balance. Marks › Colours is in Settings; the daily follows it.

Things that look wrong and aren't.
- **Saved games.** A game kept before the twist has no `read` field and was played in colours (`readOf`); the engine's own `newGame` default is colours, and the page passes `prefs.read` (default `scale`) explicitly, so every new call site must too. A weighed game always plays free (hard and strict are about colours), and equations are always colours (`readFor`).
- **One pack per reading.** `packOf(lex, len, "scale")` shares the colour pack's arrays and its words and sets `read`; `codeAt` looks at `P.read`, so the solvers needed no change. The kept word grades (`assay-grades.js`, regenerate with `node assay-grades.mjs` when the word list or answer rules change) are colour grades and also pick the levels' words in the weighed reading.
- **Stats.** Weighed games keep their own record (`modeKey` ends `-w`); the old keys are colours'. The daily is one key (`assay:daily`, `best`, `streak`) whichever reading it was played in.
- **Ladder.** Weighed from the start; the third chapter, Colours, brings the classic marks with Hard and Strict; equations are colours. Pars are the middle score of the Steady solver over 24 words, set by hand (`node` script over `rivalPlan`; two were raised for the weighed reading).
- **Together.** The team (colours split between the players, `roleOf`) and its table stay in colours; a race follows the dealer's Marks (`read` in the match). Primes are weighed on their digits; boards mode has no strip (several strips would not fit).
- **Hidden answers.** Anywhere answers are kept (a game, a table, a race's match) they are shifted letter by letter, so a glance gives nothing away.
- **The share text** is a line a guess, ◆ right, ▲ later, ▼ earlier (colours: ◆ ◇ ·), numbered, with the date or level and the reading. Never the square emoji.
- **Keyboard** (1 November 2026, Korbi: "sometimes when typing quickly the letters don't come through, not sure if hitbox or entry related"). It was the hitbox: a tap in the gap between keys was heard by nothing. One listener on the keyboard gives a tap in a gap, or within half a rem of a key, to the nearest key (`keyAt`); a held key doesn't repeat; typing redraws only the row being typed.
- **Crude words may be guessed, never be answers** (Korbi, 1 November 2026: "crude words are allowed in [the original game] so let's include them here too (guesses not answers is good)"): `assay-crude.js`; slurs against a people, a faith or a sexuality are left out, since a team's guesses show on the partner's screen.
- **Rival** (3 November 2026, Korbi: "'against the computer' makes little sense"; "probably drop from all"): the solvers stay for measuring levels and tests, never loaded by the page.
- **Levels** (November 2026, "levels like dominos"): 41 in eight chapters, three to a row; stars are for guesses (one for solving, two within a guess of the par, three within it; a sprint counts words). Kept under `assay:track`, `assay:camp`, `assay:lruns`.
- **Numbers** (November 2026, Korbi asked for more variations): Primes (four- and five-digit primes, a guess must be prime) and Equations (a sum that balances, `assay-equations.js` fetched only when wanted). Played alone, on the ladder and in free play.

Not done: the team game is one board of one word in colours only; no weighed reading for the team's split; the strip for several boards. Not judged: whether a person (not Steady) finds the weighed reading as easy as the colours; Korbi's thumb decides.
