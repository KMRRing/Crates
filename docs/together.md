# Together
Start a party (Party, with Crates picked) or ask your partner to play from the games screen: either opens Crates in a room, and it deals a shared board. Menu → Sides, Shared board or Hidden sides, starts the other game in the same room (23 October 2026, Korbi: "start a party (or join directly if it's your partner) -> partner or anyone can join -> collaborative crate game (hidden sides) can be started"; the menu's Play together and Play hidden had gone with the room setup every game's menu gave up, so the hidden game could not be reached at all, and rooms from a party or a partner always opened shared). Either of you may change it, while nothing has been guessed on the board or after it is over (`mode` in `coop.js`: it deals a shared board at once, or empties the room's board for the hidden lobby, where each sets up a side and presses Ready; both lose their picks, the team's tally stays); mid-board it says to finish the board first. Party's Crates seats two, so a guest joins by Party's invitation like the partner. Your partner opens the room and joins. The team shares 4 lives and 4 clues per board. A duo match always deals countries (30 October 2026, Korbi: "make it so multiplayer crates always is countries": a commodity is a specialist's answer, and two people at a table share what a country calls to mind): `POOL` in `coop-room.js`, the pure part of `coop.js` (what a room holds, `tidy` and `newBoard`; `tests/coop.mjs` runs it in Node). A room made before keeps a `pool` of commodities or mixed in the database, and is read as countries from its next board on; no pool is chosen in a room, and the settings sheet there lists only the countries' topics and regions. Solo Crates is as it was, with all three pools. Opened in a room, the page shows the rete until the room's board is drawn, never your own board meanwhile (October 2026; your own board used to be drawn first, so for the first seconds you and your partner were on different boards); joining reads only Crates' part of the room, not the whole room, with that part held while joining so the joining transaction takes one round trip, not two.

One rule runs through both modes: you never see the result of your own action, your partner does.
- Your wrong guess: you see a life go, your partner sees whether it was one away (in Hidden, also whose pick is the odd one out). Everyone keeps their picks after a wrong guess.
- Your clue (Together): your partner reads the explanation and sees the crate colour. In Hidden, clues work differently (below).
- A crate you find: your partner names it.

**Together** — both of you see all sixteen words and each other's picks. Whoever started the game sets the settings.

**Hidden** — each of you sees only your own eight words; your partner's show as blank sealed tiles, and it's up to you to describe which one you mean. Both screens lay the tiles out identically. Every crate holds one to three words from each side, so a crate always needs picks from both of you.
- Before the first board you each set up your own side: your difficulty shapes only the eight words dealt to you (the topics are those of whoever deals the board, Settings' own). Which countries can come up is shared and set by whoever started the game. The board is dealt once you've both pressed Ready.
- Clues come from the shared pool of four and are spent on tiles that are sealed on your screen: tap one, then its ?. You see that tile's explanation and crate colour (its ? takes the colour); your partner, who can see the word, only sees that a clue was used on it. The rest is up to the two of you.
