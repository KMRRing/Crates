# Firmament: facts to open against a primary source

Written from memory on 4 November 2026 without a connected library. Each line is in a lesson as worded and should be read against its source before release; anything doubtful was left out.

- Chinese court sunspot records "over a hundred before Galileo" (Yau and Stephenson 1988); Han annals 28 BCE.
- Spring and Autumn Annals "about three dozen" eclipses; Shiji comet of 240 BCE as Halley's.
- Babylonian Diaries 652 to 61 BCE (Sachs and Hunger); 19-year cycle by about 500 BCE; Venus tablet chronology argument.
- Egyptian Sirius invisible about 70 days; decan star clocks 2100 to 1800 BCE.
- Hōkūle'a 1976 distance (more than 4,000 km), 1980 voyage navigated by Nainoa Thompson; 2020 DNA study (Ioannidis et al.); settlement dates.
- Maya: Dresden Codex copy date; Venus 584 correction; 405-month eclipse table.
- Eratosthenes: 252,000 variant; Egyptian 157.5 m and Attic 185 m stadia; Cleomedes as source.
- Hipparchus: Spica about 2 degrees; "at least 1 degree per century"; only surviving work the Aratus commentary.
- Antikythera: cell counts 235 (5 turns) and 223 (4 turns); dating range; 2021 UCL planetary model.
- India: 121 verses; Madhava and Nilakantha dates; Nilakantha model of 1500; Jantar Mantar founding dates, 27 m Samrat Yantra.
- Ma'mun's degree (56 2/3 miles) and the spread of readings; al-Biruni about 6,340 km and 34 arcminute dip.
- Ulugh Beg: 1,018 stars, 40 m sextant, sidereal year 6 h 10 m 8 s.
- Taqi al-Din observatory 1577 to 1580 and the causes given for its demolition.
- Su Song tower height and fate; Guo Shoujing 27 stations, Shoushi 1281 to 1644.
- Nabta Playa dating and alignments (Wendorf and Malville 1998); Dogon claim (Griaule and Dieterlen 1950, van Beek 1991).
- Chapters 4 to 6: Copernicus's 1514 circulation and Rheticus 1539; Osiander's preface; Index dates 1616, 1620, 1835; Tycho's accuracy about one arcminute; mercury study 2010; Horrocks's 1639 date; Harriot's drawing 26 July 1609 and six-fold lens; Lipperhey, Metius, Janssen claims; Marius's December 1609 claim; Gan De and Xi Zezong 1981; Han Shu sunspot 28 BCE; the 1616 Bellarmine certificate against the unsigned memo; Huygens's about 220,000 km/s; Hooke 1679 letter; Lepaute's share of the 1758 sums; Palitzsch's sighting; Cavendish 5.45 density; Challis's two sightings.
- Chapters 7 to 9: Titius–Bode terms and 1766 date; Le Gentil's years; transit station counts and Lalande's 8.6 arcsec of 1771; Bradley's Gamma Draconis in 1725 to 1728; Henderson 1832 to 1839, Struve's Vega figure and its later revision; Fraunhofer 574 lines; Janssen at Guntur 18 August 1868 and Lockyer's October date; Huggins's 1868 Sirius figure; Fleming's 10,000 stars; Cannon's 350,000; Payne 1925 and Russell 1929; Kelvin 1862; 1054 durations; Chandrasekhar 1930 voyage and 1935 meeting; Mercury's 530 and 43 arcsec; Lescarbault 1859; Príncipe 1.6 and Sobral 1.98 and 0.86; Earman and Glymour 1980, Kennefick 2009; radio test 0.01%; GW150914 masses.
