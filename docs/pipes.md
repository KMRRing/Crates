# Gears
Wind a watch's train: every level is a plate and a hand of whole gears that must all fit one train from the mainspring to the dial, with stars. Every square holds a **single** gear (teeth to all four edges), a **duo** (two small gears meshed inside the square) or a **trio** (three); only the two end gears of a duo or trio reach out, one to each of two sides (straight or round a corner). Gears are always drawn whole, never clipped. Gears was Pipes until 4 November 2026, from Pipe Dream (1989) by way of a fitting puzzle.

## Rules
- Power leaves a barrel (clockwise, speed 1); every wheel gets one exact speed (a fraction): a mesh gives the next wheel the opposite way at equal tooth rate, an arbor shares one speed, a ring keeps the way. A loop that comes back with another way (odd loop) or another speed (ratios that do not multiply to 1: a **bind**) is a **jam**.
- Tooth sizes (modules 4, 5, 6) never mesh: two on one layer **clash**; wheels on different layers ignore each other. Two trains must not mesh at all (**tangle**), even at one size.
- A duo, trio or single keeps the tooth rate; only a stack (wheel and pinion on one arbor), a riser or a ring changes it. A goal wants a way and an exact speed.
- Solved: every piece laid, no jam, clash, tangle or stray, every train one path from its barrel to its sink through its pinned pieces, every goal met, every click satisfied. A **click** takes a single wheel which must turn the way shown.
- Book I (the old forty) never turns pieces; Books II on do (tap a held tile or a laid piece). Stars as before: a turn of a laid piece counts as a correction.

## Traps
- A level's `id` is its identity: stars (`pipes:trainstars`) and runs (`pipes:train`) are kept by it. Levels are `w<chapter><n>`; new levels go at the end of a chapter with a new id; a run keeps a signature of its level and starts over if that changed. Levels are written by `node pipes-train-pick.mjs`, not by hand; the tests re-solve all of them and want the listed answers and no others. The 4 November levels replaced the old forty (`g<chapter><n>`, keys `pipes:puzzle`, `pipes:stars`): their content was different, so those stars are deleted at start-up rather than carried over.
- A wheel of n teeth at module m has pitch radius m n / 2, and ports sit at the middle of an edge, tangent whatever the sizes (so ratios, compound arbors and layers fit one 60-unit square). `tests/pipes-gear.mjs` checks every catalogue piece. Teeth are 4 or more by design (legible on a phone).
- Elbow trios are drawn so the end gears don't touch: three gears all touching would be an odd loop, a jam inside the piece.
- The solver counts answers by distinct pieces on distinct squares, and prunes with the same jam rule as play; a level may list several answers (early chapters), and `layRoute` writes a route as N E S W (single), n e s w (duo), ^ > v < (trio).
- Teeth phase: `phasesOf` turns each wheel from the one it was reached by so a tooth lies in a gap; a closed loop can leave a pair slightly off (a picture only). The solved train turns with the Web Animations API (each wheel's duration is set by its tooth count, and every wheel's animation is set to the same clock, `spinEpoch`: start one later, or redraw from zero, and meshed teeth drift apart or jump; the mainspring and the load's wheel need a direction class like any other, or they never animate); the board draws the ground first and the wheels over all of it so a tooth reaching into the next square is not painted over. No SVG filters (Safari blurs them). Only Chromium has been looked at.
- The 3D map's old-model engine (`pipes-puzzle*.js`, `pipes-map-*.js`, `pipes-draw.js`'s old drawing) is kept whole and tested (`tests/pipes-puzzle.mjs`, `tests/pipes-map.mjs`); its saved keys `pipes:map`, `pipes:mapstars` are never touched. `pipes-pieces.js` and parts of `pipes-draw.js` are shared with the new engine.
- The sound is synthesised (`pipes-sound.js`, no files), the menu's Sound toggle (`pipes:prefs`), and silent in a watching frame; a new jam is two dull clacks.
- Levels of Books II on are written by `node pipes-gear-pick.mjs` (the old forty come from `pipes-train-levels.js` through `pipes-gear-legacy.js`), the daily pool by `node pipes-gear-pool-pick.mjs`; `tests/pipes-gear-levels.mjs` solves every one again. Ids are the identity of stars, so a chapter is only re-picked before it ships.
- A mirrored plate (`transformLevel`) keeps every direction (the barrel is always clockwise), but a pinned piece's goal names a wheel by index and a mirrored kind may list its wheels the other way round: the goal's index is remapped there. A few elbow duos have no mirror image; the pool only holds plates all eight of whose turns exist.
- Today's plate is a pool plate under a turn or mirror chosen by the day (`pipes-gear-daily.js`); nothing is solved on the page. It is kept in `pipes:daily` by the day (stars) and its run under the id `d<day>`, which is pruned when another plate opens.
