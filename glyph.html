Fields

How to play

Fill the board so every across and down run is a real word. Each coloured field has a secret rule about letters, and the colour tells you what kind:

Blues and greens judge each letter on its own. Yellows and oranges judge neighbouring pairs, read left to right and top to bottom; their marks sit on the joins. Purples and pinks (dashed) judge the whole field at once, when every cell is filled.

Tap a cell to pick its word (tap again to switch direction), type, and press Place. Every placement counts toward par, so use them as experiments.

Two checks per board show which of your placed letters match our fill: a solid dot matches, a hollow ring isn't in our fill. Any fill that obeys every rule wins, ours or yours.
